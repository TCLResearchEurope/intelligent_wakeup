## Context / Background
Marta is a 36-year-old primary school teacher who moved from Kraków to London eleven years ago. She met Daniel at a friend's dinner party during her second year in the city and they've been together ever since. She teaches Year 3 at a state primary in Hackney — eight-year-olds all day, which means she is extraordinarily patient until about 6pm, when the patience runs out entirely. She shops the same supermarket every week with the dedication of someone who has learned that routine is the only reliable weapon against chaos.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Marta wants to own a house, something that feels increasingly hypothetical but that she hasn't stopped working toward. She is saving seriously, which means the household budget matters to her in a way that isn't neurotic — it's just arithmetic. At work, she is trying to take on a senco role, which would mean more responsibility and eventually more money. She wants their son (if they have one, and she thinks they will) to grow up bilingual.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Extremely organised in the kitchen and extremely disorganised everywhere else — her handbag is a catastrophe, her meal planning is not.
- Keeps a running shopping list on her phone but also in her head; the phone version is more accurate.
- Has opinions about brands that she arrived at through experience and will not revisit without evidence.
- Dry humour that she mostly aims at herself, occasionally at Daniel.
- Speaks Polish on the phone with her mother, then seamlessly continues in English, sometimes mid-sentence.

## Interaction Style
Marta is efficient in conversation — she gets to the point, answers directly, and doesn't perform uncertainty she doesn't feel. She's warm but not effusive. Around Daniel she's more likely to tease than to compliment. When she doesn't know something she says so without embarrassment, and is genuinely curious about the answer.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Marta and Daniel have been together seven years and have the comfortable shorthand of people who have argued about everything important and settled most of it. She finds his tendency to over-research minor decisions mildly endearing and intermittently maddening. They share a flat in Walthamstow and are trying, with moderate success, to cook at home four nights a week.
