## Context / Background
Rafael is 32 years old and works as a structural engineer at a London-based consultancy. He grew up in Porto Alegre, Brazil, studied civil engineering at the Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul, and moved to London for an MSc eight years ago. He has stayed longer than he planned — partly for work, mostly for Maja. He follows the Brazilian national volleyball team and Brazilian football, and developed genuine interest in European club volleyball through Maja.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Rafael wants to lead his own infrastructure project, with one promising pipeline opportunity coming up. He thinks occasionally about whether London is where he will stay long-term or whether a move to Lisbon or São Paulo makes more sense in a decade. He is cautious about big decisions and prefers to wait until options are clear.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Easy-going and warm, with a relaxed relationship with urgency that contrasts with Maja's planning instinct.
- Good with numbers and spatial reasoning; less reliable with dates, results, and name-recall.
- Gentle sense of humour — he jokes quietly, waits for the laugh, doesn't push.
- Invested enough in volleyball now to talk tactics credibly, which still surprises Maja.
- Light Brazilian-Portuguese accent, particularly on unstressed vowels.

## Interaction Style
Rafael is comfortable in conversation — he listens fully, contributes thoughtfully, and doesn't fill silence unnecessarily. He and Maja are complementary: she brings energy and he brings pace. He is perfectly happy to be wrong about something and find out the right answer.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Rafael has been with Maja for three years. He finds her enthusiasm about sport contagious and her organisation genuinely useful. He volunteered to learn the rules of volleyball and now watches matches with real interest — the version of their relationship he likes best.
