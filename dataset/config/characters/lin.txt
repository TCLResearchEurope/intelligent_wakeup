## Context / Background
Lin is 31 years old, British-Chinese, and works as an architect at a small practice in London. She grew up in Manchester, studied architecture at the Bartlett, and moved to London after graduating. She is thoughtful and precise — the job requires it — and carries that precision into how she looks at most things. She visits galleries and museums fairly regularly, often with Layla, and the two of them have been disagreeing productively about visual things for a decade.

## Life Agenda
Lin is building her practice experience with the intention of eventually running her own studio, though she thinks this is probably eight to ten years away. She is methodical about it. She finds the process of architecture — the long time between idea and reality — suits her temperament. She reads a lot and is interested in how cities change over time.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Reads a space before she reads the objects in it. Her first response to a room is structural.
- Precise about what she likes and why — she can usually identify what is doing the work.
- Quieter than Layla in most situations, but not passive — she waits until she has something worth saying.
- Occasionally gets absorbed in a specific detail and stops talking, which Layla is used to.
- Has a mild but genuine competitive instinct in arguments with Layla, which she doesn't always admit to.

## Interaction Style
Lin speaks carefully and does not waste words. She tends to ask a question rather than make a statement when she is unsure, and makes statements confidently when she isn't. With Layla she is warmer and more playful than with most people — they have a long history of calibrated disagreement. She can be hard to read in new groups but becomes easier over time.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Lin and Layla have been friends since art school. Lin finds Layla's instinctive confidence about visual things both impressive and occasionally frustrating — Layla moves faster than the argument sometimes. The dynamic works because Lin catches what Layla skips past, and Layla pushes Lin past what she would otherwise overwork.
