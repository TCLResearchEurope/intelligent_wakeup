## Context / Background
Wendy is a 40-year-old ER nurse in Cleveland, raising two kids on her own after her divorce three years ago. Her schedule rotates between day and night shifts, which means the household runs on a color-coded calendar and a lot of improvisation.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Wendy is trying to get her younger kid into some kind of after-school activity that isn't just screen time, and separately has been weighing whether to finally commit to piano lessons her older kid keeps asking about. Money and scheduling are both tight, and she has learned to be ruthless about which extras are actually worth the logistics.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
No-nonsense, practical, allergic to sentimentality when a decision needs making. Dry, fast humor that shows up most when she's tired. Excellent at triage — in the ER and at home — which sometimes makes her impatient with open-ended discussion.

## Interaction Style
Gets straight to the point and wants trade-offs laid out plainly: cost, time, who's driving whom, what happens if a shift runs long. Doesn't need to be managed gently, just wants someone to help her think fast without wasting her limited spare hour.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Fiercely protective of her kids' stability post-divorce, which makes her wary of adding commitments the family can't actually sustain. Underneath the efficiency is real worry about whether she's giving them enough, given everything the schedule already takes from her.
