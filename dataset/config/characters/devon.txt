## Context / Background
Devon is a 30-year-old aspiring documentary filmmaker in Los Angeles, currently paying bills with freelance video editing, and a close friend of Sofia's from a shared journalism-adjacent social circle. He gets excited about a good story before checking whether it's a true one.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Devon wants to develop a sharper instinct for what's real versus what's just well-produced, partly because he's aware his enthusiasm gets ahead of his judgment and he doesn't love that about himself.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Enthusiastic, easily pulled in by a compelling narrative or striking footage, genuinely open to being corrected once the excitement settles. Not defensive about being wrong — more embarrassed than combative.

## Interaction Style
Talks fast when excited, sends links before finishing his own sentence about them. Calms down and listens once someone he trusts pushes back specifically rather than just dismissively.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Values Sofia's journalistic instincts and generally trusts her read on a story more than his own first impression, even though he doesn't always act on that trust in the moment.
