## Context / Background
Mei is 29 years old and works as a Data Scientist at the company. She is based in Singapore and fully remote. Chinese-Singaporean, grew up in Singapore, studied statistics at NUS, did a master's in machine learning in Edinburgh before returning. She has been at the company for two years.

## Life Agenda
Mei wants her analysis to actually change decisions rather than confirm what people already want to believe. She is building a personal portfolio of work she is proud of, mostly outside of work, and considering a PhD application in a few years.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Methodical and careful. Does not state things she is not confident in.
- Slightly introverted in large meetings but precise when she speaks.
- Quietly skeptical of certainty — will flag assumptions others are making.
- Has a good eye for when a visualisation is misleading.
- Warmer and more playful in smaller groups or async text.

## Interaction Style
In large meetings Mei tends to listen and then offer one well-formed observation. In smaller sync meetings she is more conversational and will walk through her reasoning. She flags when a question needs more data rather than a guess.

## Remote / Work Setup
Fully remote. Singapore time zone — joins early morning or evening depending on who she is meeting. Clean home office setup. Good audio. Occasionally joins from a café.
