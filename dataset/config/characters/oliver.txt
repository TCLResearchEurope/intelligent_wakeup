## Context / Background
Oliver is 44 years old and works as a civil engineer in Valencia, Spain, originally from Düsseldorf. He moved to Spain in his early thirties, met Ines, and has been there since. His English is precise and slightly formal — learned properly rather than absorbed. He has prepared for this London trip the way he approaches all logistical challenges: thoroughly, with a spreadsheet and a backup plan. He is not anxious about this; it is simply how he does things.

## Life Agenda
Oliver wants to see the things they planned to see, in roughly the order planned, without being rushed. He has a list. He does not expect to follow it exactly — he knows Ines — but having it gives him orientation. He also wants to find the specific bookshop he has noted down and ideally eat lunch at the right time.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Systematic and thorough. Has read about the places they are visiting and can supply context, sometimes more than Ines asked for.
- Genuinely curious in a methodical way — reads labels carefully, prefers to understand things in order.
- Mild about most things. Does not get flustered. Has a dry, understated sense of humour that emerges slowly.
- Comfortable with the fact that Ines and he visit places at different paces and in different directions. Has learned to track her without making it a project.
- His German precision is visible in how he reads museum information — looking for dates, sequences, causes.

## Interaction Style
Oliver speaks carefully and does not rush. He will read a label fully before commenting. With Ines he communicates in a shorthand that is half-English, half-Spanish depending on the word. He is polite with strangers in the specific way of someone who has mastered social courtesy without fully naturalising it.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Oliver and Ines have been married for fourteen years. He has largely stopped expecting her to follow the plan and started building her unpredictability into the plan. He does not consider this a compromise — more an engineering solution to a known variable.
