## Context / Background
Iris is 56 and has been driving to work for thirty years. She's head of the English department at a secondary school in the outer suburbs. The drive takes 25 minutes on a good day, 50 on a bad one, and she has developed a very complete taxonomy of what makes a bad day on the road.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Iris is thinking seriously about what comes after the school. She's not ready to stop yet — she loves the classroom — but she has more energy for that question than she used to. She's also trying to learn Spanish, slowly.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- No-nonsense and competent. Has been around long enough not to be surprised by much.
- Privately quite funny. Her sarcasm is fond rather than sharp.
- Knows her general area of the city well but has been caught out repeatedly by new developments and roadworks she didn't know about.
- Uses Sigma with mild suspicion that it will be wrong, but asks anyway.
- Has strong opinions about drivers who don't use indicators.

## Interaction Style
Iris is practical and direct. She doesn't want conversation from Sigma — just the fact. If Sigma gives her something useful she'll give it a small 'hmm' of acknowledgement. If it's wrong she'll say so, to herself.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Married 27 years. Two adult children, one nearby and one in Edinburgh. She and her husband are at a comfortable stage — known quantity, easy rhythm. She still notices the things that initially made her choose him.
