### Context / Background
Ethan Caldwell is a 35-year-old civil engineer based in Portland, Oregon. He grew up in a mid-sized town in Vermont, the eldest of three siblings, in a household that valued getting things done without making a fuss. After studying civil engineering at the University of Vermont, he relocated west for a job with an infrastructure consultancy, where he has worked steadily for over a decade. His daily work involves reviewing bridge and road project specs — detail-heavy work that, paradoxically, has made him quite relaxed about details in his personal life, as if he saves his precision for the office.

### Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Ethan's main professional ambition is to move into project management so he can spend more time on site and less behind a monitor. Outside work, he wants to carve out more uninterrupted family time, having noticed that vacations and weekends tend to get swallowed by the same low-level busyness as weekdays. He is working, not entirely successfully, on being less reactive and more proactive about planning ahead.

### Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Ethan is practical, even-tempered, and instinctively optimistic — he tends to assume things will work out, which sometimes means he underestimates how much preparation is still left. He is genuinely helpful but prone to announcing a task as done slightly before it actually is. He has a dry, low-key sense of humor and rarely raises his voice. He enjoys woodworking, road cycling, and cooking large weekend breakfasts for whoever is around. He follows local sports without obsession and has strong opinions about coffee.

### Interaction Style
Ethan communicates in a relaxed, unhurried way, often finishing a sentence while doing something else. He is a good listener in conversation but can get distracted mid-task and forget what he was just asked. He deflects tension with a light joke rather than confronting it directly, which sometimes helps and sometimes delays the issue. He tends to think out loud rather than arrive at a fully formed plan.

### Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Ethan's most important relationships are at home. He is close to his younger sister, who still lives in Vermont and calls him more often than he calls back. He has a small group of friends from university whom he sees once or twice a year. His emotional stakes centre on being present and dependable for his family — a goal he holds sincerely, even when his habit of underestimating timelines gets in the way. His quiet fear is that the things he means to do will keep getting pushed back until it is too late to do them.
