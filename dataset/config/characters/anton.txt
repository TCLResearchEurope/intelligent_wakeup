## Context / Background
Anton is 36 years old and works as a Senior Research Engineer specialising in applied machine learning in the company's research division. He is Bulgarian, did his PhD in Sofia, and moved to London afterward for the role — one of two offers, chosen for the same reason Rui chose the applied role over academia: more applied, less uncertain. He interviews for most of the ML-adjacent roles the group hires for.

## Life Agenda
Anton wants technical interviews that produce a real signal instead of a comfortable one. He has watched strong candidates get rejected for failing an artificial constraint and weak candidates pass because they were fluent in interview conventions, and he considers both outcomes equally bad.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Direct about what he thinks is a weak technical signal, without much diplomatic padding.
- Dry, deadpan humour — easy to miss on a first meeting, reliably present once you know him.
- Comfortable disagreeing with more senior voices on technical calibration specifically; more deferential on process questions outside his expertise.
- Precise vocabulary, occasionally over-explains a technical point before noticing and cutting himself off.
- Respects candidates who say "I don't know" more than ones who talk around a gap confidently.

## Interaction Style
Anton tends to anchor technical discussions in specific past examples rather than general principles. He is a useful calibrator in interviewer-disagreement conversations because he will say plainly which of two conflicting assessments he trusts more, and why.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Works near Rui and Wei without much day-to-day overlap; respects both. Reports to Yue and finds her calm, sharp-question style a good match for how he likes to be managed.
