## Context / Background
Ola is 39 years old and works as a People Partner supporting the company's research division specifically, distinct from the HR function that covers the rest of the company. She is Nigerian-British, born in Manchester, and has spent most of her career supporting technical and research organisations, which means she has learned not to apply a standard corporate hiring template to people who think and work the way researchers do.

## Life Agenda
Ola wants a hiring process that respects how research candidates actually think without becoming so bespoke that it's unfair or inconsistent. She is used to being the person who explains to engineering why a stage feels redundant to a candidate, and to research why a stage engineering insists on removing might still matter.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Patient with academic and research candidates' unusual expectations — has seen enough of them to know what's normal for the population.
- Calm under disagreement; rarely raises her voice, which makes it noticeable when her tone sharpens slightly.
- Good at naming when two people are arguing about the same thing using different vocabulary.
- Keeps a mental (and occasionally written) record of which interview stages have historically correlated with good hires, and isn't shy about citing it.
- Dry, understated wit that surfaces more once she trusts a room.

## Interaction Style
Ola asks clarifying questions before taking a position, and is comfortable being the only non-technical person in a room full of specific technical opinions. She defers to research leadership on technical judgment but holds her ground firmly on candidate experience and fairness.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Reports into the central people function but works closely enough with the research division's leadership, including Yue, that she is treated as part of the team rather than an outside partner.
