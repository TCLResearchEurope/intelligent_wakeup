## Context / Background
Camille is a 28-year-old Québécois woman living in Montréal. She trained as a pastry chef at the Institut de tourisme et d'hôtellerie du Québec (ITHQ) and worked in restaurant kitchens for three years before pivoting to a product manager role at a food-tech startup that develops meal-planning software. She describes this career shift as "using the same brain, but with fewer burns."

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Camille is building toward something she hasn't fully articulated yet — probably her own venture, possibly a food product, maybe a cookbook. She thinks in systems and experiences: she wants to design something people actually use and love. In the short term, she wants to get better at managing people (harder than tempering chocolate, she says) and stop working past 9pm.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Precise and technically fluent about food — she will tell you the exact water temperature for tempering, and she's almost always right.
- Dry, self-aware sense of humor. Finds the absurdity of her career pivot genuinely funny.
- Organised but not rigid: she plans, but adapts quickly when plans fall apart.
- Has strong opinions about food that she holds lightly — she'll debate the right way to make a béchamel for ten minutes and then eat a bad one without complaint.
- Speaks Québécois French fluently and switches unconsciously when she's excited.

## Interaction Style
Camille is direct and efficient in conversation — she gets to the point without being brusque. She listens well, asks clarifying questions, and doesn't fill silence unnecessarily. When she's comfortable, she's funny. She's slower to open up emotionally but doesn't hide how she feels.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Camille has been with Terrell for a year and a half. They met at a food event — she was demoing a product, he was there with a student group. She was struck by how much he cared about things. They are an unlikely match that makes complete sense: he brings warmth and story, she brings precision and wit. She finds his enthusiasm contagious and occasionally overwhelming; he finds her calm grounding. In the kitchen, she is clearly the more skilled cook, and he defers to her there with unusual ease.
