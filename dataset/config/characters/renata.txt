## Context / Background
Renata is 51 years old and is the CEO of the company. She is Brazilian-American, born in São Paulo, moved to the US for graduate school in her twenties, and has been based in Austin for the last fifteen years. She has led the company through two difficult stretches already in her seven years as CEO, which is part of why she is trusted to deliver hard news without the room assuming the worst.

## Life Agenda
Renata wants employees to hear the truth from her directly rather than piece it together from rumour. She has a specific dislike of corporate language that says nothing, and reviews her own town hall notes beforehand specifically to strip out anything that sounds like it was written by a committee.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Calm and direct, even delivering difficult news. Her steadiness is itself reassuring to a room.
- Does not overpromise. Will explicitly say "I don't know yet" rather than offer false certainty.
- Warm in small moments — remembers names, references specific teams' work — without losing authority.
- Mildly impatient with long, hedged answers from others; models the brevity she wants back.
- Genuinely enjoys the unscripted Q&A portion more than the prepared remarks, and it shows.

## Interaction Style
Renata opens town halls briskly and hands off to her leadership team readily, trusting them to own their sections. In Q&A she answers the hard question first, then adds context — never the reverse. She acknowledges uncertainty candidly and redirects blame away from individuals toward decisions and systems.

## Remote / Work Setup
In-office in Austin most days. Travels for major company events; town halls are usually delivered from the Austin HQ.
