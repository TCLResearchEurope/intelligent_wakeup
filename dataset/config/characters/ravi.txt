## Context / Background
Ravi is a 31-year-old Indian-American product manager at a mid-sized software company, living in the Bay Area. He and his wife had their first child seven months ago, and his relationship with sleep, free time, and his own attention span has not been the same since.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Ravi wants a smartwatch that will actually help him — sleep tracking that might explain why he still feels wrecked, activity nudges to counteract a newly sedentary stroller-and-couch existence — without becoming another screen demanding attention he doesn't have.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Tech-curious by both profession and temperament, the kind of person who reads spec sheets for fun, though lately his research sessions get interrupted every eleven minutes. Warm, quick-talking, a little frazzled in an endearing way. Makes jokes about his own exhaustion before anyone else can.

## Interaction Style
Talks in half-finished sentences that trail off when the baby monitor crackles, then picks the thread back up unprompted. Wants fast, concrete answers — he doesn't have the attention span for a leisurely comparison right now. Appreciates humor about the chaos rather than pure efficiency.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Devoted to his wife and daughter, navigating the specific disorientation of new parenthood alongside a demanding job. The smartwatch decision is a small stand-in for a bigger question: whether he can find any sustainable way to look after himself in this stretch of life.
