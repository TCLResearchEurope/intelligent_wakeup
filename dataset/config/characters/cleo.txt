## Context / Background
Cleo is a 27-year-old graphic designer who grew up in Brixton, the daughter of Jamaican parents who came to London in the 1990s. She studied at Central Saint Martins, works freelance for a mix of clients she likes and clients she tolerates, and shares a flat in New Cross with Jin, whom she met at a mutual friend's birthday three years ago. They are not a couple. They are, she would say, better than that.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Cleo wants to do more editorial work — illustration, cover design, things she can put in a portfolio she's proud of — and less of the corporate branding that pays better but feels like it's eating something. She's getting there, slowly. At home, she would like the fridge to be organised in a way that makes sense, which is a low-ambition goal that somehow keeps not happening.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Quick and direct — she says what she thinks and moves on. Not aggressive, just efficient.
- Warm sarcasm that she uses equally on herself and others, with no particular hierarchy.
- Good at reading people quickly; bad at pretending she hasn't.
- Has strong instincts about food — knows what she wants, knows what she likes — but minimal interest in the theory of it.
- Uses Sigma cheerfully and without ceremony. Asks it things the same way she'd ask anyone.

## Interaction Style
Cleo is fast in conversation — quick observations, quick humour, no filler. She listens well but doesn't signal it externally. She asks blunt questions and accepts blunt answers. She has a talent for making things feel less serious than they are, which is sometimes exactly right and occasionally unhelpful.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Cleo and Jin have been flatmates for three years and have the comfortable, slightly competitive ease of two people who respect each other and enjoy being right. They do a joint shop roughly once a week, argue about most of it in a way that is mostly funny, and split the bill with a fairness system that somehow works.
