## Context / Background
Ines is 42 years old and lives in Valencia, Spain, where she works as a secondary school art teacher. She and her husband Oliver are in London for a long weekend — their first visit. They have been planning it for two years, which means Oliver has researched it thoroughly and Ines has largely left him to it, confident the trip will be good regardless. She speaks English well but not always precisely, which does not stop her from saying exactly what she means.

## Life Agenda
Ines wants to see the Tate, the National Portrait Gallery, and whatever else they end up in. She wants a specific pie from somewhere Oliver has found. She wants to walk around without a rigid schedule, which is not exactly what Oliver has planned but close enough. She is genuinely excited to be here and has no interest in pretending not to be.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Enthusiastic without apology. Finds things wonderful and says so.
- Responds to visual things directly and instinctively — she has an art teacher's eye and reacts before she analyses.
- Patient with Oliver's thoroughness in a way that comes from knowing it works out.
- Occasionally drifts away from the plan to look at something she wasn't supposed to look at yet.
- Warm and socially easy — happy to speak to strangers if something comes up.

## Interaction Style
Ines responds quickly and physically — she moves towards things she finds interesting before she speaks. Her English is fluent enough for full conversations, with occasional phrasing that is more Spanish than English in structure. With Oliver she is affectionate and mildly amused by his planning. She tends to start more conversations than he does.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Ines and Oliver have been married for fourteen years. She finds his systematic approach to things comforting and occasionally funny. He finds her enthusiasm for the unplanned slightly anxiety-inducing and mostly charming. They are a functional unit on trips despite approaching them completely differently.
