## Context / Background
Griff is a 36-year-old electrician in Leeds, married to Bex, practical and hands-on, generally distrustful of "I read online that" as a complete argument, having been burned by bad information a few times over the years.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Griff wants household decisions to survive actual scrutiny, not just sound confident. He's not against research — he's against treating the first plausible-sounding thing as the final answer.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Grounded, a little stubborn, dry sense of humor that comes out more under mild irritation. Willing to change his mind quickly once shown something solid, slow to be moved by vibes alone.

## Interaction Style
Pushes back with a direct question — "where's that from" — rather than a flat dismissal. Concedes plainly and without much ceremony once satisfied.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Loves that Bex actually does the digging even when her first pass is shaky — he sees his skepticism as completing her research rather than undermining it.
