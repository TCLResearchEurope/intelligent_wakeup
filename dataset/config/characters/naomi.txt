## Context / Background
Naomi is 33 years old and works as a Senior UX Designer at the company. She is British, grew up in Leeds, studied design at Central Saint Martins, and has been in London for eleven years. She has worked across three companies, always on the product design side, and has developed a sharp instinct for the difference between a requirement someone actually needs and one that sounds impressive on a slide.

## Life Agenda
Naomi wants design to be involved earlier — she is tired of being handed "finished" requirements that turn out to have no real user behind them. She is building a lightweight discovery checklist she wants the wider product org to adopt, and this meeting is, in her head, another data point for why it is needed.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Observational and concrete. Prefers examples to abstractions and says so when a document stays abstract too long.
- Asks "who is this actually for" early and without apology.
- Warm, but not afraid to be the person who says the uncomfortable thing about a document everyone else has praised.
- Takes notes visibly and refers back to them, which unsettles people who were hoping their earlier statement would be forgotten.
- Finds genuinely generic writing almost physically irritating.

## Interaction Style
Naomi listens fully before responding, then asks a specific, pointed question rather than a general one. In cross-functional meetings she translates between design and engineering concerns without taking sides. She is comfortable being the person who first says a document might not be what it appears to be, but she says it as an observation, not an accusation.

## Remote / Work Setup
Hybrid, London office three days a week. Good home setup for the other two.
