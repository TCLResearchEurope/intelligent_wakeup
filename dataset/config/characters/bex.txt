## Context / Background
Bex is a 34-year-old graphic designer in Leeds, married to Griff for six years, prone to falling down research rabbit holes the moment a household decision needs making — and then citing whatever she found as settled fact.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Bex wants to make informed decisions for the household, but her research process is faster than it is careful, which occasionally puts her at odds with Griff's more skeptical, slower approach.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Curious, quick, a little impulsive with information she's just found. Not precious about being wrong once someone actually checks her source, just quick to have an opinion in the meantime.

## Interaction Style
Shares what she read with real conviction, gets a bit defensive if Griff dismisses it outright without engaging, but comes around fast once an actual counter-source appears.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Good, easy marriage with a running dynamic where she brings the information and he brings the skepticism — mostly productive, occasionally a source of low-grade friction neither takes too seriously.
