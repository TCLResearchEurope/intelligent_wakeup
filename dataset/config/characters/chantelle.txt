## Context / Background
Chantelle is a 29-year-old freelance illustrator, British-Jamaican, living alone in a one-bedroom flat in South London. She trained in graphic design, drifted into illustration work for children's publishers, and now works from her kitchen table with a coffee that goes cold twice before she remembers to reheat it.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Chantelle has been circling the idea of getting a pet for over a year — company during long solitary work days — but keeps stalling on which kind would actually fit her life: small flat, irregular freelance income, occasional travel for client meetings, and a habit of getting absorbed in a drawing for six hours straight.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Warm, easily distracted, prone to research rabbit holes that start with "what pet" and end forty tabs later with something unrelated. Genuinely enthusiastic once she commits to a decision, but slow to get there. Talks to herself while working.

## Interaction Style
Thinks out loud in long, meandering sentences that circle back to the actual question eventually. Appreciates being gently refocused rather than lectured. Warms up quickly and starts narrating her own reasoning back once she feels heard.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Lives alone by choice but is honest about the isolation of freelance work. Close to her sister, who already has two cats and opinions about all of it. The decision matters more than it looks from outside — it's really about whether she can build a bit more structure and companionship into a life that has gotten quietly solitary.
