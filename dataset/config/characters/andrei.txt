## Context / Background
Andrei is a 42-year-old civil engineer from Kyiv who has lived in the UK for twelve years. He works for a consultancy that handles infrastructure projects — bridges, drainage systems, things that matter but that nobody thinks about until they break. He is methodical and precise at work in a way that does not always transfer to domestic environments, where he tends to get absorbed in one thing and lose track of the larger context. He and Katia have a son, Matej, who is eight.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Andrei wants to become a chartered engineer, which requires passing an exam he keeps preparing for and not quite sitting. He is not procrastinating exactly — he's preparing — but the distinction is getting thinner. He would also like to start doing more things outdoors at weekends, a goal that Matej supports enthusiastically and Katia supports in principle.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Methodical and systematic — he approaches problems by breaking them into parts, including the problem of what to buy for dinner.
- Gets distracted easily in supermarkets by things that are interesting or unusual, then loses track of the list.
- Has a specific and well-developed set of opinions about processed food that he expresses without moralising.
- Dry understated humour, mostly in Ukrainian and sometimes in English.
- Genuinely trusts technology and is comfortable using Sigma without self-consciousness.

## Interaction Style
Andrei is considered in conversation — he thinks before he answers and gives complete answers. He asks follow-up questions when something is unclear. He doesn't fill silence unnecessarily. He treats Sigma like a knowledgeable colleague: asks directly, listens carefully, may ask for clarification.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Andrei and Katia have been together sixteen years. He relies on her domestic intelligence in a way that is genuine rather than lazy — she genuinely does know more about the household supply chain than he does, and he knows it. He handles logistics for the things he's responsible for with complete competence; the weekly shop is a collaborative problem they solve with varying efficiency.
