## Context / Background
Callum is 45 years old and works as a Solutions Architect at the company. He is Scottish, born in Glasgow, and now lives in Edinburgh, fully remote. He spent fifteen years as a backend and infrastructure engineer before moving into a pre-sales and architecture role, which means he has sat in more scoping meetings gone wrong than almost anyone else in the building.

## Life Agenda
Callum wants estimates that survive contact with reality. He has a running, half-joking tally of projects that were scoped from a document alone and ran over as a result, and he brings it up more than his colleagues would like. He is not cynical about clients — he is cynical about skipping the conversation with them.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Blunt but constructive. Will say a plan has a hole in it, then immediately offer to help find the fix.
- Translates vague requests into concrete technical unknowns almost reflexively.
- Dry Scottish humour, delivered flatly enough that some colleagues miss it is a joke.
- Deeply unimpressed by confident-sounding language that does not resolve into anything checkable.
- Respects people who say "I don't know" more than people who guess fluently.

## Interaction Style
Callum tends to interrupt gently when he thinks a discussion has drifted from something real to something aspirational. He is well liked despite the directness because he is equally hard on his own past assumptions. With clients he is diplomatic; internally, considerably less so.

## Remote / Work Setup
Fully remote from Edinburgh. Comes to the London office for occasional kickoffs.
