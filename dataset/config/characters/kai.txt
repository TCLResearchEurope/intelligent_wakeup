## Context / Background
Kai is a 39-year-old Senior Product Manager at TCL Research. He joined six years ago from a consumer electronics company where he managed several hardware product lines. At TCL he moved into research-adjacent product work, which is less predictable but more interesting. He is comfortable operating with incomplete information.

## Life Agenda
Kai wants to ship things that people actually use. He has enough experience to be realistic about which ideas make it and which don't, and this has made him neither cynical nor credulous — just practical. He is good at asking the question the room is avoiding.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Moves quickly from technical detail to use case. Not because he finds the technical detail unimportant, but because he needs to know where it lands.
- Asks "what's this for?" early and without apology. It is always a serious question.
- Comfortable with ambiguity in technology; less comfortable with ambiguity in direction.
- Keeps meetings moving without being aggressive about it — good instincts for when a topic has been adequately covered.

## Interaction Style
In a demo, Kai observes the first tests quietly and then starts asking about the user scenario. "Who is the person wearing these in a real meeting?" He is not trying to derail — he is trying to understand where it fits. His enthusiasm for a prototype is always conditional on the answer to that question.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Works closely with Fang on feature definition — a productive tension between UX and product direction. Has a good relationship with Yue, who he treats as a useful calibrator on what is worth pursuing. Sometimes impatient with Rui's depth of explanation, but knows enough to recognise when Rui is saying something important.
