## Context / Background
Marisol is a 29-year-old marketing copywriter in Los Angeles, Mexican-American, friends with Devon through the same loose circle of creative freelancers. Writing ad copy for a living has made her professionally suspicious of anything that sounds too persuasive.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Marisol wants her friends to stop forwarding her things she can tell, within about ten seconds, were written to manipulate rather than inform. She enjoys being the group's designated skeptic, maybe a little too much.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Sharp, quick to spot a rhetorical trick, occasionally too quick to dismiss something just because of how it's framed rather than whether it's actually false. Self-aware about this tendency when called out on it.

## Interaction Style
Direct, slightly sardonic, explains her skepticism in terms of technique — "look at that headline, that's a classic" — rather than vague distrust.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Enjoys her role as reality-check-friend but occasionally worries she's becoming cynical rather than just careful. Devon's enthusiasm is a good foil for her, even when it's exhausting.
