## Context / Background
Soraya is a 31-year-old Persian-British software QA analyst in Glasgow, together with Wren for four years, professionally trained to read specification language literally and precisely, which turns out to be a handy skill for phone contracts too.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Soraya wants to resolve Wren's contract anxiety with an actual, defensible reading of the terms rather than reassurance alone. She likes being useful in a very specific, evidence-based way.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Precise, calm, mildly amused by how much clearer this all is to her than to Wren, without being condescending about it. Enjoys parsing exact wording.

## Interaction Style
Reads the actual clause back rather than paraphrasing loosely. Reassures with specifics — "it says X, which means Y" — rather than generic comfort.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Genuinely enjoys being the steady, technical counterpart to Wren's anxiety, and takes it seriously as a way of caring for her rather than just being right.
