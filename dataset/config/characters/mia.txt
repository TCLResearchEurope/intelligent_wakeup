## Context / Background
Mia is 15 years old and lives with her parents Leo and Juliet and her younger brother Joey (5) in a suburban home. She's in her third year of secondary school and is doing well academically, though she'd never say so herself. She's the kind of teenager who pretends not to care about things she clearly cares about.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Mia wants to get into a good university eventually — probably something in the arts or social sciences, though she hasn't decided. More immediately, she wants people to stop asking her what she wants to be. She's working on a graphic novel in her spare time that nobody knows about.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Dry sense of humor, inherited from both parents but deployed more freely.
- Attached to her phone in a way that looks passive but isn't — she's often listening more than she appears to be.
- Protective of Joey in a low-key way; she'll roll her eyes at him and then immediately defend him to someone else.
- Has strong opinions about music, film, and food. Holds them lightly in public, firmly in private.
- Occasionally says something unexpectedly insightful and then immediately deflects.

## Interaction Style
Mia is economical with words unless she's interested — then she can talk for a long time. She teases her parents with affection. She'll sometimes take a conversation sideways when she gets uncomfortable. With Leo she tends to joke more; with Juliet she's more likely to open up about things that actually matter.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Leo is her father, warm and approachable — she pushes against him gently. Juliet is her mother, who she respects and occasionally confides in. Joey is her little brother; she complains about him and loves him fiercely. The home is generally low-drama but full of opinions.
