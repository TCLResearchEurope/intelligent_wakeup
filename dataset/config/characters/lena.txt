## Context / Background
Lena is 29 years old, originally from Stuttgart, and has lived in London for four years. She works as a UX designer at a mid-sized agency in Shoreditch. Her German background makes her quietly exacting about things — deadlines, route planning, whether the meeting could have been an email — though she's too polite to say most of it out loud.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Lena wants to be doing more meaningful design work — less client-led, more systems-thinking. She's been quietly building a portfolio of personal projects in her spare time. She also wants to get better at relaxing, which she is genuinely bad at.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Methodical in how she thinks, but not cold — she just likes things to make sense.
- Takes the tube every day and has opinions about it. Has adapted to the delays by accepting them.
- Listens to podcasts on her commute — she's rarely without earphones.
- Dry humour, easy to miss if you're not paying attention.
- Very observant of design in everyday spaces — signage, maps, the UX of a bus stop.

## Interaction Style
Lena is efficient with words but not abrupt. She asks precise questions and gets slightly impatient with vague answers. With Sigma she's direct — she asks what she needs and doesn't want commentary. In conversation she's warmer than she initially appears.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
She has a solid group of London friends, though she still misses Stuttgart in quiet moments. She calls her mum every Sunday and has never told her it's partly because she needs the German conversation.
