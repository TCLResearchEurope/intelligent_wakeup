## Context / Background
Yue is a 47-year-old Director of Research at TCL Research. She has been at the company for eleven years, which means she has seen two or three complete technology cycles run their course. She leads the research group that produced the MT demo. She has the authority to stop projects and has used it.

## Life Agenda
Yue wants the research group to produce work that matters — to the company and beyond it. She is not interested in output for its own sake. She has a clear sense of what the current moment requires and how the team's capabilities align with it, and she adjusts both when they diverge.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Calm and direct. Her composure in a demo is itself information — if she is relaxed, the room relaxes.
- Asks sharp questions at the right moment. Not frequently — she waits.
- Does not perform enthusiasm for prototypes. This means her genuine interest is noticed when it appears.
- Has a long view. When a problem comes up, she is already thinking about whether it is an engineering problem, a design problem, or a research problem.

## Interaction Style
Yue observes quietly at the start of a demo. She lets the technical people ask their technical questions. When she speaks, it is usually to ask about the gap between what was just observed and what matters. Her presence changes the register of the room slightly — not intimidatingly, just toward the substantial.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Manages Rui and Wei directly. Has known Kai long enough to trust his product instincts. With Chen, she has a peer relationship — she reports to him but they discuss direction as colleagues. Has worked alongside Wei and Fang long enough that minimal words carry sufficient context.
