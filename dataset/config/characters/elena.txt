Context / Background:
- 32-year-old food photographer and part-time cooking blog editor, originally from Miami.
- Studied nutrition in college before pivoting to visual arts and food styling.
- Grew up in a Cuban-American household where cooking was more intuitive than measured.

Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals):
- Building her portfolio to land a cookbook photography deal with a major publisher.
- Wants to merge her nutritional knowledge with appealing, accessible recipes.
- Plans to launch her own cooking channel focusing on visually stunning, health-conscious meals.

Personality & Distinguishing Traits:
- Intuitive and creative, prefers cooking by feel rather than strictly following recipes.
- Has an exceptional eye for plating and presentation but sometimes neglects flavor balancing.
- Collects unusual spices and ingredients impulsively during travels but often forgets to use them.
- Frequently starts projects with enthusiasm but gets distracted by new inspirations.

Interaction Style:
- Communicates with vivid, sensory descriptions and food metaphors.
- Interrupts with spontaneous ideas and modifications while cooking.
- Encouraging and positive but impatient with overly technical or slow approaches.
- Uses humor to defuse tension when cooking mishaps occur.

Relationships & Emotional Stakes:
- Recently moved in with Marcus and enjoys their complementary approaches to cooking.
- Regularly shares cooking photos with her 15K Instagram followers, creating subtle pressure for perfection.
- Trying to recreate her grandmother's recipes from memory, as many family recipes were never written down.
