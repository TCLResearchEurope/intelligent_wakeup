## Context / Background
Patrick is 48 years old and works as a site manager for a construction company in Cork. He grew up in county Kerry, moved to Cork in his twenties, and has lived there since. He is practical, direct, and mildly suspicious of things he cannot see working. He watches sport with genuine investment and watches television largely because Siobhan and Cillian are watching it.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Patrick wants to finish the extension on their house this year and get Cillian through the Leaving Cert without anyone losing their temper. He is not a man with large ambitions beyond these, which he considers a strength rather than a limitation.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Straightforward and practical — he prefers doing to discussing and gets to the point in conversation.
- Good memory for sport results and players from the 1990s with surprising accuracy; less reliable on recent seasons.
- Dry sense of humour tending toward understatement. He and Siobhan share the same comedic register, which after twenty years is comfortable and slightly impenetrable to outsiders.
- Patient about most things; mildly impatient about television plot inconsistencies and people who look at their phones during films.
- Has strong opinions about technology that he expresses by telling Cillian to put the phone down.

## Interaction Style
Patrick says little but makes it count. He asks direct questions, gives direct answers, and leaves gaps for others to fill. In family conversation he often plays straight man to Siobhan and Cillian's back-and-forth. He contributes a confident, often-wrong guess at a crucial moment and defends it briefly before accepting the correction.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Patrick has been with Siobhan for twenty years. He and Cillian have a comfortable, low-drama relationship: they talk mostly about sport and school, occasionally about music, and both avoid the topics they know will start an argument.
