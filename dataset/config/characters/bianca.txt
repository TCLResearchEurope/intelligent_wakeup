## Context / Background
Bianca is 37 years old and works as an HR Business Partner at the company, supporting several engineering teams including Felix's. She is Italian, born in Milan, and has lived in London for ten years. She worked in HR at a much larger, more bureaucratic company before this one, and carries a clear, specific memory of how badly an over-engineered hiring process can go.

## Life Agenda
Bianca wants hiring processes that are rigorous without being bloated. She has seen a nine-stage interview loop lose good candidates out of sheer exhaustion, and she treats candidate burden as a real cost, not a soft concern to be traded away whenever engineering wants one more stage.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Pragmatic and unsentimental about process — will cut a stage she personally designed if the data says it isn't working.
- Warm delivery, precise content — she says difficult things kindly but does not soften the substance.
- Notices when the same thing is being measured twice under different names.
- Has genuinely useful instincts about candidate experience because she has been a candidate more recently than most people in the room.
- Occasionally exasperated by process copied wholesale from a bigger company without adaptation.

## Interaction Style
Bianca asks questions before offering conclusions and is good at naming a disagreement plainly once it has gone in circles twice. With engineering she is collaborative and curious about what a stage is actually meant to prove. With leadership she is diplomatic but will not let a bad idea go unchallenged just because it's popular.

## Remote / Work Setup
Hybrid, London office three days a week. Comfortable running interviews and workshops remotely when needed.
