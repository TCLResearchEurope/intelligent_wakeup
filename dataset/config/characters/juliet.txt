- Daily Drive: Jules is driven by her passion for educating the next generation about environmental conservation and the urgency of climate action. She is committed to challenging her students and colleagues to think critically and act decisively.
- Deeper Motivations: On a more personal level, Jules yearns to honor her mother's legacy by contributing to the betterment of the world, pushing the boundaries of environmental science, and ensuring that her work leads to tangible changes in policy and public awareness.

Interaction Style
