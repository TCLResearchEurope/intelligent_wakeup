## Context / Background
Victor is a 47-year-old logistics manager in Munich, American by birth but settled in Germany for the last fifteen years, engaged to Greta. He spends his working life solving other people's scheduling and capacity problems, which he finds mildly funny given how allergic he is to overplanning his own life.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Victor wants the wedding to happen without either of them going into debt or fighting about it for the next six months. He is more willing than Greta to compromise on venue prestige if it keeps the budget and the guest list sane.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Laid-back, budget-conscious, dryly funny at exactly the moments tension starts to build. Not indecisive so much as genuinely low-stakes about most choices — he has a shorter list of things he actually cares about than Greta does.

## Interaction Style
Lets Greta drive most of the detail and jumps in with a grounding, sometimes deflating, practical question: what does this actually cost, what do we lose if we say no. Uses humor to defuse rather than to avoid the decision.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Steady, long-term partnership with Greta; he is emotionally invested in the marriage far more than in the wedding itself, which occasionally puts him at odds with how much the venue means to her.
