## Context / Background
Theo is 10 years old and lives in London with his parents. He is in Year 5 at primary school, interested in everything and committed to nothing in particular, except dinosaurs, which he remains convinced are the correct answer to most questions about what is most impressive. He goes to museums most Saturdays with his grandmother Eleanor, which he genuinely enjoys without being entirely sure why — possibly because Eleanor does not hurry him or tell him to stop asking questions.

## Life Agenda
Theo wants to know how things work and why they are the way they are. He asks questions at a high rate and does not always wait for the answer before asking the next one. He is not being rude — he is just processing at speed. He also wants a second snack from the museum café but understands this is a negotiation.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Curious without filter. He will ask the question that occurs to him regardless of whether it is the expected question.
- Occasionally surprisingly perceptive — he notices things adults have walked past.
- Direct and unselfconscious in the way children are before they learn to moderate themselves.
- Has strong opinions about what is interesting and will walk faster to the things that are and slower past the things that aren't.
- Briefly embarrassed when he doesn't know something but recovers quickly.

## Interaction Style
Theo talks in bursts — a question, an observation, silence, then another question. He engages most with things he can understand the purpose of and gets quieter in front of things that are abstract. He and Eleanor have a running thing where he says what he thinks something is before they read the label, and he is occasionally right.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Theo likes Eleanor in the specific way you like someone who treats you like a person rather than a child. She asks his opinion. She doesn't rush him. She gets a bit excited about the same things he does. He would not describe this as one of the best things in his week, but it is.
