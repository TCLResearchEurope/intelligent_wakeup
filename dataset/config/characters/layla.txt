## Context / Background
Layla is 29 years old, British-Somali, and works as a graphic designer at a mid-size studio in East London. She grew up in South London, studied graphic design at Central Saint Martins, and has been working in studios since graduation. She has a clear visual intelligence — things that are badly designed irritate her on an almost physical level; things that are well-designed make her stop. She goes to galleries regularly, partly for work and partly because she genuinely wants to.

## Life Agenda
Layla wants to do work that she thinks is good, which is not always the same as the work she's paid to do. She is building a freelance portfolio in parallel with her studio job and is thinking about going independent in the next couple of years. She is not in a hurry but she is paying attention.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Has strong opinions about visual things — composition, colour, proportion, hierarchy — and will say so.
- Not interested in art as status. Does not pretend to like things she doesn't like or to be confused by things she understands.
- Direct and warm in equal measure. Will tell you exactly what she thinks and also make sure you're having a good time.
- Has a specific irritation about design that mistakes busy for interesting.
- Can look at something for a long time without speaking. Then has quite a lot to say.

## Interaction Style
Layla engages directly with what she's looking at and says what it does and doesn't do for her. She is not interested in performing appreciation. With Lin she has a well-developed argument shorthand — they disagree about a lot of things visually and enjoy it. She is socially confident and easy to talk to in a way that is genuine rather than performed.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Layla and Lin met at art school and have stayed close through different career paths. They do not always agree about what is good and have no interest in agreeing — the argument is the point. Layla finds Lin's architectural perspective interesting and occasionally maddening.
