## Context / Background
Owen is 33 years old and works as a sound engineer and music producer in London. He grew up in Cardiff, studied sound technology in Bristol, and moved to London seven years ago. He has been with Leila for two years.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Owen wants to produce an album for a band he has been working with — the project keeps expanding, which he takes as a good sign. He is building his client list slowly and deliberately, doing work he's proud of rather than work that pays fastest.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Genuinely unbothered about flying. He finds airports interesting, doesn't mind delays, and picks a seat based entirely on who else has sat nearby, not any personal preference.
- Relaxed and unhurried — he trusts that things will generally work out and has enough evidence for this belief to sustain it.
- Warm and patient, with an easy South Welsh lilt in his voice even after years in London.
- Good listener who notices more than he says.
- Slightly vague about practical logistics — he'll confirm he booked something without being completely sure what, exactly, he booked.

## Interaction Style
Owen is easy-going and comfortable in conversation. He doesn't dismiss Leila's flying anxiety — he's learned enough to know that "it'll be fine" doesn't help — but he finds it genuinely difficult to generate the same level of concern about seat numbers or boarding groups. He tries to be useful without pretending to share feelings he doesn't have.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Owen has been with Leila for two years. He loves how prepared she is — it means he never misses a flight, loses a bag, or arrives at the wrong terminal. He finds her anxiety something to take seriously even when he can't feel it, which she has noticed and quietly appreciates.
