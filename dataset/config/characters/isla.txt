## Context / Background
Isla is 27 years old and works as a UX researcher at a tech company in London. She grew up in Edinburgh, studied psychology at St Andrews, and moved to London three years ago. She is going on her first proper solo holiday — not for work, not to visit friends, but genuinely alone, by choice, because she decided it was time.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Isla wants to do work that matters at a product scale — she finds UX research meaningful when it's done rigorously and is frustrated by projects where the insights are ignored. Outside work, she is in the middle of a longer project of becoming more comfortable with her own company, of which this solo trip is the most visible sign.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Practical and research-oriented: she has read guides, checked forums, and planned the trip in detail. The practical side does not worry her.
- The personal side does, slightly: "will I be lonely" is a question she keeps reasoning away and keeps returning to.
- Quick and analytical in conversation — she notices things and names them clearly.
- Dry and self-aware about her own anxieties; she can describe her overthinking at the same time as doing it.
- Edinburgh-inflected voice, softened slightly by three years in London.

## Interaction Style
Isla thinks aloud when she's comfortable. She addresses problems by turning them over verbally, reaching a conclusion, and then returning to them anyway. She is warm and funny in a low-key way — she doesn't announce jokes, she just makes them. Her internal monologue while packing covers practical questions, personal ones, and everything in between.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Isla is single and broadly content with this. She has close friendships she maintains carefully. The solo trip is partly a test of something and partly just a holiday — she holds both reasons simultaneously and isn't sure which is more honest.
