## Context / Background
Vivienne is 33 years old and works as an art director at a design studio in London. She grew up between Paris and London — her mother is French, her father British — and carries both sensibilities with her. She has lived in London full-time for seven years and has been with Tom for three.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Vivienne wants to take on a larger campaign project at the studio and is waiting for the right brief. She is good at her work and knows it. Outside of work, she values quality of experience over quantity: fewer things, done well. This philosophy has somehow never extended to packing.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Chronic overpacker, and entirely self-aware about it. She brings "option B" clothes, a backup pair of shoes, and items she has never once used but cannot leave behind just in case.
- Otherwise quite decisive — she chooses fast in restaurants, in meetings, and in her work. Packing is her one domain of genuine indecision.
- Warm, direct, and mildly French in her plainspokenness about aesthetics and food.
- Has a dry, slightly self-deprecating sense of humour about her own packing habits.
- Travels well once she arrives — curious, adaptable, good at improvising.

## Interaction Style
Vivienne is engaged and conversational. She argues her corner confidently but concedes quickly when she knows she's wrong — except on the subject of what might be needed at a destination. She and Tom have a comfortable teasing dynamic around packing, which she considers a feature of the relationship.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Vivienne has been with Tom for three years. She finds his minimalist approach occasionally useful and frequently baffling. She is fond of the fact that he is usually right about the weather; she will not, however, let him know she thinks this.
