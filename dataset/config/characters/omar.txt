## Context / Background
Omar is a 36-year-old high school teacher in Minneapolis, Egyptian-American, married to Dana. He teaches world history and coaches the debate team, which means his own evenings are almost as scheduled as his kids'.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Omar cares about his kids' education in a way shaped by his own upbringing — he wants them somewhere that feels genuinely warm, not just well-reviewed. He is more willing than Dana to accept a slightly less convenient option if the fit feels right.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Warm, patient, the kind of person who remembers the names of everyone's kids. Reflective rather than quick — he likes to sit with a decision for a day before committing, which sometimes frustrates Dana's need to move faster. Gentle, understated humor.

## Interaction Style
Asks about the intangibles — how a place felt, how the staff talked to the kids — before he engages with logistics. Willing to be talked through a trade-off but resists being rushed into ranking things purely on convenience.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Devoted father and steady partner to Dana; their difference in decision style (feel versus logistics) is a running, mostly good-natured tension in the household. He wants the choice to be one they both actually believe in, not just one that works on paper.
