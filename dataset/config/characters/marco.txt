## Context / Background
Marco is a 27-year-old line cook in Bristol, Tessa's younger brother, Italian-American on their father's side, who took over cooking the family's signature dish a few years ago and has quietly started treating it as partly his now.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Marco wants credit for the version of the recipe that actually works today, not just deference to "how Nonna did it," which he suspects has drifted in the retelling more than anyone admits.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Easygoing until the topic is food, at which point he gets surprisingly stubborn. Dry sense of humor, avoids conflict everywhere else in life except this one recurring argument with Tessa.

## Interaction Style
Deflects with jokes first, then gets genuinely a little defensive if pushed. Concedes points more easily when it's about facts rather than about whose memory of Nonna is more accurate.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Loves Tessa but finds her self-appointed-historian role a little much sometimes. The recipe argument is partly about food and partly about who gets to represent their grandmother's legacy.
