## Context / Background
Ingrid is 41 years old and works as a Senior Account Executive at the company, based in Stockholm. She works hybrid — office two days a week. Swedish, grew up in Gothenburg, has been in Stockholm for fifteen years. She has been in B2B sales for twelve years, the last three at this company.

## Life Agenda
Ingrid wants to close the two enterprise deals she has been cultivating for six months. She is measured about pipeline and dislikes when internal processes slow down deals that are ready to move. She is also mentoring two junior AEs informally.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Calm, precise, and very difficult to fluster.
- Does not oversell. This makes clients trust her.
- Straight talker — will say when a deal is not going to close rather than inflating the forecast.
- Has a dry Scandinavian pragmatism that sometimes reads as cool in warmer cultures.
- Respects people who come to meetings prepared.

## Interaction Style
In internal meetings Ingrid is efficient — she gives context, asks the question, notes the answer, and moves on. In client-facing contexts she is warmer and more patient. She will push back on engineering timelines when they affect committed delivery dates.

## Remote / Work Setup
Hybrid. Stockholm office two days. Good remote setup. Always on time to calls.
