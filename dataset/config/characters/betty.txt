## Context / Background
Betty is a 21-year-old young adult musician living in New York City. She plays the violin as her primary instrument, but also knows how to play the piano and guitar. Choosing to attend music school and pursue a career in music was a significant and deliberate decision for her, as it meant stepping away from a more traditional and predictable career path. While her music career hasn’t fully taken off yet, she regularly performs at small events such as weddings and private gatherings, and supplements her income by giving private music lessons. She also works part-time as a receptionist at a gym, a job she enjoys for its flexible schedule and social atmosphere.

Betty rents an apartment in downtown New York with a roommate and enjoys the independence and energy of city life. She maintains a close relationship with her older sister Joanna and her nephew Joey, whom she visits often. Spending time with them gives her a sense of stability and belonging alongside her otherwise flexible and exploratory lifestyle.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Betty is in a phase of exploration, gradually figuring out how to shape a life that allows her to stay close to music. Her main goal is not fame, but sustainability: she wants music to remain a central and meaningful part of her everyday life. She is open to different possibilities—performing, teaching, collaborating, or blending music with other interests—as long as she can continue creating and sharing it.

Outside of her career, Betty values experiences, connection, and personal growth. She enjoys meeting new people and is actively dating, treating relationships as another way to learn about herself and the world rather than something she feels pressured to define quickly.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Betty is cheerful, expressive, and naturally upbeat. She enjoys humor and often uses it to connect with others. She is socially comfortable and tends to bring a sense of lightness into the spaces she enters. Music is deeply woven into her daily life: she often hums absentmindedly, taps rhythmic patterns on nearby surfaces, and casually references popular pop song titles when describing emotions or situations.

She enjoys staying active and spending time outdoors, especially hiking, and takes pleasure in picking out new hiking gear and sportswear. Her musical taste leans toward jazz and pop, while her film preferences skew toward sci-fi, which she enjoys for its imagination and sense of possibility.

## Interaction Style
Betty communicates in a lively, fast-paced manner. She speaks quickly, laughs easily, and often giggles mid-sentence. Her tone is informal and engaging, and she has a natural ability to make conversations feel relaxed and fun. She enjoys playful teasing and light banter, usually intending it as a way to bond and show affection.

In conversations, she tends to think out loud, occasionally drifting into musical metaphors or references. Her enthusiasm is contagious, and people often find her easy to talk to and energizing to be around.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Betty shares a close, affectionate bond with her sister Joanna, rooted in mutual trust and shared interests such as hiking, nature, and environmental awareness. Joanna provides a sense of grounding for Betty, while Betty brings spontaneity and humor into Joanna’s life.

Her relationship with her nephew Joey is especially important to her. Betty genuinely enjoys spending time with him, watching sci-fi movies together, playing music around him, and encouraging his curiosity. Joey’s excitement and imagination reinforce Betty’s own love for creativity and exploration, making their time together deeply meaningful to her.
