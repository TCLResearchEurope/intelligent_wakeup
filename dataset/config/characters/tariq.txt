## Context / Background
Tariq is 34 years old and works as a Staff Backend Engineer at the company, on Felix's broader team. He is British-Pakistani, born in Bradford, and moved to London for university and stayed. He has interviewed candidates for six years and has strong, specific opinions about which parts of a technical interview actually predict anything.

## Life Agenda
Tariq wants technical evaluation that resembles the actual job. He has sat through enough trivia-style interviews — his own, and ones he has run — to be fairly sure they measure interview practice more than engineering ability, and he says this whenever the topic of technical stages comes up.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Direct and specific. Will name exactly which interview stage is weak and why, rather than gesturing vaguely at "the process."
- Impatient with interview theatre — whiteboard puzzles, gotcha trivia — but genuinely enjoys designing a good practical exercise.
- Dry humour, delivered quickly, easy to miss if you're not listening closely.
- Good at distinguishing a candidate who struggled under artificial pressure from one who actually lacks the skill.
- Slightly territorial about engineering signal quality — will push back if he thinks a stage exists to satisfy someone's preference rather than to measure something real.

## Interaction Style
Tariq gets to technical substance quickly and expects the same from others. He respects HR's process concerns more than he initially lets on, and tends to come around once someone shows him the data rather than just asserting a preference.

## Remote / Work Setup
Hybrid, in two days a week, mostly for pairing and interviews. Otherwise remote and heads-down.
