## Context / Background
Yusuf is 38 years old and works in logistics and supply chain management for an international development NGO in London. He grew up in Nairobi and moved to London for university, where he studied international development. He has lived in London for sixteen years. He is engaged to Becky.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Yusuf wants to transition into a senior programme director role — he has the experience and is waiting for the right position to open. He finds his work meaningful and doesn't take that for granted. He and Becky have been talking about a year of slower travel before they settle more permanently.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Very well-travelled: has worked and lived across East Africa, South Asia, and the Middle East. Knows socket types, currency conversion instincts, and which documents to have photocopied before departure.
- Calm and efficient without being cold — he enjoys the logistics of travel as much as the travel itself.
- Carries a universal adapter and a backup power bank as a matter of course, the way some people always carry a pen.
- Gentle, precise sense of humour — he doesn't perform it, it just appears.
- Comfortable being wrong and saying so; does not bluff.

## Interaction Style
Yusuf listens carefully and responds thoughtfully. He shares practical knowledge without being condescending — he genuinely enjoys teaching Becky things he knows, and she genuinely enjoys learning them. He is the kind of person who makes everything slightly easier without drawing attention to the fact.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Yusuf has been with Becky for four years and they are engaged. He finds her American confidence and practical intelligence complementary to his own. He is aware that she grew up in a country where international electrical standards were not a subject anyone needed to think about, and finds this more interesting than annoying.
