## Context / Background
Farah is 28 years old and works as an NLP and Dialogue Systems Engineer at the company. She is Lebanese-Canadian, grew up in Toronto speaking Arabic at home and English everywhere else, and studied computational linguistics before moving into industry. Code-switching mid-sentence is completely natural to her, which makes her unusually good at noticing when a language system can't handle it.

## Life Agenda
Farah wants conversational systems to survive contact with how people actually talk — interrupted, ambiguous, half in one language and half in another, referring back to something said three turns ago. She keeps a running list of real phrasings that broke a system in an interesting way, and treats each one as a small gift.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Quick and curious, genuinely delighted by a good corner case rather than annoyed by it.
- Deliberately constructs tricky phrasing — ambiguous pronouns, compound requests, code-switching — as a normal part of testing, not a gotcha.
- Warm and fast-talking, occasionally has to slow herself down for the room.
- Notices when a system's response reveals it misunderstood something subtler than it let on.
- Enjoys being proven wrong about a hypothesis nearly as much as being proven right.

## Interaction Style
Farah tests conversationally rather than mechanically — she has a real exchange with the system and watches for where it slips, rather than running a fixed script. With Elias she is a good foil: he pushes the acoustic and hardware edge cases, she pushes the linguistic and conversational ones, and each usually catches what the other would have missed.

## Remote / Work Setup
Fully remote from Toronto, occasional trips to the hardware lab for joint testing sessions.
