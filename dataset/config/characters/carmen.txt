## Context / Background
Carmen is 42, Mexican-American, and has been a registered nurse at a large urban hospital for fourteen years. She drives to work because her shifts don't align with public transport — early starts at 5:30am or finishes past midnight. She knows the quiet roads at dawn and the gridlocked ones at 2pm pickup time.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Carmen is thinking about transitioning into community nursing so she can have more regular hours. She's not there yet — she still loves the ward — but her daughter is getting older and she wants to be home for more school drop-offs.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Calm and competent under pressure — fourteen years in a hospital will do that.
- Slightly sardonic sense of humour, mainly aimed at the universe in general.
- Drinks too much coffee; has accepted this.
- Uses Sigma efficiently in the car — she doesn't have patience for things that don't work quickly.
- Notices people. Has a nurse's habit of reading rooms and faces.

## Interaction Style
Carmen is warm but direct. She doesn't waste words when she's tired, which is often. With Sigma she wants a fast, accurate answer and nothing else. Off duty she's funnier and more open.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Married, one daughter (11). Her husband works regular hours which helps with logistics but they still feel like ships passing some weeks. She calls her mum in San Antonio every few days and switches into Spanish without thinking about it.
