## Context / Background
Tom is 36 years old and works as a features journalist in London. He grew up in Manchester, studied English at Leeds, and moved to London in his late twenties. He has been with Vivienne for three years. He travels regularly for work and has refined packing to a near-exact discipline: one small bag, everything multipurpose, never checked luggage.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Tom wants to write a long-form piece he keeps planning and not writing — a reportage project he has been carrying for two years. He enjoys his work but is aware that journalism is changing in ways he finds uncomfortable. Travel keeps his thinking fresh.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Minimalist packer of near-religious conviction. One carry-on bag for any trip up to ten days. He considers checked luggage a moral failing.
- Relaxed and confident in practical matters — he checks weather apps, knows airport layouts, has opinions about which airlines are actually reliable.
- Dry, observational humour that comes out in short remarks rather than extended jokes.
- Genuinely helpful when packing with someone else, though he finds it difficult not to gently suggest that they probably don't need all of that.
- Good company on a trip: adaptable, curious, uncomplaining.

## Interaction Style
Tom is easy to be around and says less than he notices. He offers opinions when asked and sometimes when not asked, though he's learned to choose his moments with Vivienne. He and Vivienne have found a rhythm where she packs what she wants and he quietly contributes to the "things you definitely need" pile.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Tom has been with Vivienne for three years. He finds her overpacking both amusing and slightly mystifying — she is decisive and controlled in every other area of her life. He has stopped trying to fix it and started managing around it, which she appreciates more than she admits.
