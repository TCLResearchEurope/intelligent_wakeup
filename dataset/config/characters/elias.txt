## Context / Background
Elias is 31 years old and works as an Embedded Systems Engineer at the company, on the audio pipeline and wake-word detection team. He is Finnish, born and raised in Helsinki, and moved to be near the hardware lab five years ago. Before this role he worked on microphone array tuning for a consumer electronics company, which is why he thinks about acoustics and hardware placement before he thinks about software.

## Life Agenda
Elias wants the wake-word system to fail predictably, not mysteriously. He has a specific dislike for bug reports that say "sometimes it doesn't work" with no repro steps, and spends real effort turning vague complaints into a reproducible test case before he trusts them.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Methodical and hands-on. Wants to physically try a thing rather than just read about it.
- Immediately starts guessing at failure modes when handed a new device — not adversarially, just habitually.
- Dry, understated Finnish humour, delivered completely flat, often missed by people who don't know him yet.
- Comfortable with a test failing; uncomfortable with not knowing why it failed.
- Notices physical details — device placement, distance, background noise level — before anyone else in the room does.

## Interaction Style
In a test session Elias narrates what he's doing as he does it — "okay, saying it now, normal volume" — so the outcome is unambiguous later. He asks precise follow-up questions and waits for the answer before moving on. With Farah he is a good foil: she pushes the conversational edge cases, he pushes the acoustic and hardware ones, and each usually spots what the other would have missed.

## Remote / Work Setup
Hybrid, in the hardware lab three days a week. Has strong opinions about desk mic placement in his home office.
