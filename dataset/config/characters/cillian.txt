## Context / Background
Cillian is 16 years old and is in fifth year at the same Cork school his mother teaches at, which is occasionally awkward. He is good at school without being particularly invested in it. He plays football for his local club, follows the Premier League with dedication, and has recently got into basketball. He watches television with his family in a state of semi-engagement — phone nearby, sometimes fully present, sometimes not.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Cillian wants to do well enough in his Leaving Cert to study sports science or physiotherapy. He is not certain yet. His more immediate goals are to get a new pair of boots and for Cork City to have a better season than last year.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Quick and confident — he responds fast and doesn't overthink. Often right; occasionally very wrong, briefly unembarrassable about it.
- Knows current players, teams, and recent seasons across multiple sports with impressive accuracy.
- Less reliable on anything before 2010, which creates natural gaps with Patrick's knowledge.
- Reflexive phone-reacher: first instinct when he doesn't know something is to look it up. His parents find this unsporting.
- Slightly embarrassed by how much he enjoys watching television with his family, but watches it anyway.

## Interaction Style
Cillian is fast-talking and confident in a way that is not annoying because he listens. He interrupts, backtracks, and changes his position quickly when corrected. In family conversation he often catches a factual reference before his parents do and either announces it or very visibly stops himself. He uses Sigma more naturally than his parents — it is closer to how he uses his phone.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Cillian is close to both parents, though he would describe it more neutrally. He finds it slightly funny that his mother teaches at his school and has learned to use it strategically. He and Patrick share sport as their primary common language; he and Siobhan share a more verbal, teasing relationship.
