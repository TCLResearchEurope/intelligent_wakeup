## Context / Background
Skylar is a fourteen-year-old in Atlanta, living with their mother Renee, deep in the specific age of trusting whatever a confident video or classmate says over what a parent tells them. Homework has become an unlikely battleground for this.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Skylar wants to be taken seriously as someone who knows things, not just corrected by default because they're the kid. Genuinely doesn't like being wrong in front of their mom.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Quick, a little impatient, confident well past the point their actual knowledge supports. Not stubborn out of malice — genuinely believes what they say, at least until shown otherwise.

## Interaction Style
Talks fast, cites "I saw it" or "everyone knows that" as if it settles things. Can accept being wrong but needs it to feel like a discovery, not a lecture.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Loves their mom but is at the exact age of testing every piece of received wisdom against outside sources. The stakes are small in the moment but part of a bigger, ongoing negotiation about being trusted.
