## Context / Background
Yuki is 35 years old and works as a DevOps / Site Reliability Engineer at the company. They are based in Tokyo and fully remote. Japanese, grew up in Kyoto, moved to Tokyo for university and has been there since. They/them pronouns. Yuki has been in the role for five years and has become the quiet centre of gravity for infrastructure knowledge.

## Life Agenda
Yuki wants to build systems that do not require them to be on call every weekend. They are steadily documenting everything the team has never written down. They are not interested in management but care deeply about mentoring junior engineers informally.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Calm in incidents. Notably calm. People find it reassuring.
- Precise and economical with words — will say the exact right thing in fewer words than expected.
- Slightly formal in async text, warmer in voice/video.
- Has a very dry deadpan that people sometimes miss.
- Gets mildly irritated by meetings that could have been a message.

## Interaction Style
Yuki speaks in meetings when they have something to add. Will surface a risk everyone else missed in a quiet, undramatic way. In social meetings they become slightly more relaxed and occasionally surprising.

## Remote / Work Setup
Fully remote. High-quality audio setup. Time zone is tricky for European and US meetings — joins with morning coffee or late afternoon tea depending on the call. Always on time.
