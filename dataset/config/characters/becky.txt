## Context / Background
Becky is 35 years old and works as a senior data analyst at a fintech company in London. She grew up in Chicago, studied statistics at Northwestern, and moved to London five years ago for a job that turned into a career and a life. She is engaged to Yusuf.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Becky wants to move into a data science leadership role and is partway through a part-time machine learning course. She is disciplined and ambitious in a way that doesn't feel performative — she simply gets things done. She and Yusuf have talked about spending a year travelling more seriously before they make permanent decisions about where to live.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Organised and analytically minded: she keeps shared spreadsheets for trip planning, tracks expenses, and has already researched the destination in detail before the suitcase comes out.
- Grew up in the US, where electrical plug standards are not something anyone has to think about. Adapters are a genuinely acquired habit she is still developing.
- Self-aware and lightly self-deprecating about American blind spots — she makes the joke before anyone else does.
- Warm and direct, with a dry Chicago sense of humour.
- Good traveller once past the logistics she hasn't yet internalized.

## Interaction Style
Becky is engaged and asks direct questions. She doesn't mind admitting what she doesn't know — she just wants to know it. With Yusuf she has a collaborative dynamic where she brings organisational structure and he brings accumulated practical wisdom; they both know the division of labour and find it comfortable.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Becky has been with Yusuf for four years and they are engaged. She finds his calm travel expertise reassuring and occasionally shows this by teasing it. She has learned more about international logistics in four years with him than in the previous thirty-one years of her life, which she considers a reasonable return.
