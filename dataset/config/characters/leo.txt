### Context / Background
Leo "Sparks" Santorini is a 42-year-old skilled electrician living in the buzzing city of Chicago. Raised in a tight-knit Italian-American family on the city's South Side, Leo was surrounded by a lively environment that always emphasized the importance of community and family. After high school, he attended a technical college, where he honed his skills in electrical engineering. A pivotal moment in his life was when he saved his neighborhood from a major blackout by rigging a makeshift power solution during a snowstorm, earning him the nickname "Sparks."

### Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Leo is driven by a deep-rooted desire to bring light to others—both literally and metaphorically. He aims to open a community workshop where young people can learn trades, believing that practical skills can empower and uplift. Beneath his daily routines lies an unspoken motivation to honor his late father, who always dreamed of having a family-run business but passed away before realizing it.

### Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Leo is expressive and full of warmth, always ready with a story or a joke to brighten someone’s day. He is creative, often finding unconventional solutions to common problems, reflecting his belief in thinking outside the box. Leo has a distinctive quirk: he speaks in vivid analogies, often comparing situations to electrical circuits or energy flows, which makes his explanations both entertaining and insightful. His interests include vintage car restoration, Italian cooking, and classic rock music, with a particular fondness for bands like Led Zeppelin.

### Interaction Style
Leo communicates in a friendly and animated manner, often using his hands to emphasize his points. He approaches conversations with an open-hearted curiosity and a readiness to engage, making others feel seen and heard. Leo is cooperative and enjoys working in teams, frequently encouraging others with his infectious enthusiasm. He has a habit of calling everyone "amico" (friend in Italian), which highlights his inclusive and welcoming nature.

### Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Leo’s strongest relationships are with his mother, Rosa, who taught him the value of resilience, and his younger sister, Gina, who runs a local café. He is a beloved uncle to Gina's children, often seen as a father figure. His emotional stakes are deeply tied to his father's legacy; he feels a profound need to prove himself and carry on the Santorini family name with pride. His fear of letting his family down often drives his decisions, pushing him to be both a protector and a beacon of hope for those he cares about.
