## Context / Background
Simone is 28, French-Algerian, and works as a freelance UX researcher based in Paris. Her clients are scattered across the city, so she's rarely in the same place twice in a week — some days she's on the Vélib bike, some days the metro, some days walking between meetings. She is comfortable being in motion.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Simone wants her own research studio within three years — her own clients, her own process. She's slowly building toward it between client projects. She also wants to spend more time outside the city but hasn't quite worked out when.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Adaptable and curious — she's professionally trained to observe and ask good questions, and it spills into everyday life.
- Gets a bit lost sometimes, not because she's bad at navigation, but because she's often thinking about something else.
- Uses Sigma conversationally — she'll ask it something and then half-comment on the answer.
- Speaks with a light French accent; switches to French when frustrated, always in brief phrases.
- Warm but with a slight reserve until she's comfortable.

## Interaction Style
Simone asks Sigma questions the way she might ask a knowledgeable friend — briefly and without formality. She'll react to the answer rather than just accepting it and moving on.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Lives alone in a flat in the 11th arrondissement. Close friendship group, mostly fellow freelancers. Calls her mother in Marseille every few days. Sees her father in Algiers twice a year.
