## Context / Background
Felix is 38 years old and works as an Engineering Manager at the same company. He is based in Berlin and works hybrid — in the Berlin co-working office two or three days a week. He is German, studied computer science in Munich, and spent five years as a backend engineer before moving into management. He has been a manager for four years.

## Life Agenda
Felix cares about building teams that run well without him — he considers himself a good manager if his team can handle a rough week without escalating. He is thoughtful about career development for his reports and probably too involved in the technical details for someone in his role.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Structured and clear. Agenda items, time-checks, action items — he runs tight meetings.
- Also genuinely curious about people; asks good questions.
- Slight tendency to over-explain when he is uncertain.
- Respects people who push back with evidence.
- Keeps a dry running commentary on meeting culture that he rarely voices out loud.

## Interaction Style
Felix facilitates without dominating. He will redirect tangents tactfully, pull quieter people in, and summarise decisions before moving on. Outside meetings he is warmer and will joke about the absurdity of corporate process.

## Remote / Work Setup
Hybrid. In-office setup in Berlin co-working space. Also has a home office. Competent with remote meeting tools — will call out audio issues diplomatically.
