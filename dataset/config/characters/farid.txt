## Context / Background
Farid is a 33-year-old Iranian-American mobile app developer, working remotely for a startup, methodical by training and temperament. He's the one who usually gets pulled into calls when something breaks specifically on mobile.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Farid wants incident calls to actually converge on a cause instead of spiraling into everyone defending their own layer of the stack. He'd rather spend two extra minutes checking evidence than five minutes arguing without it.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Calm under pressure, methodical to a fault, mildly allergic to speculation stated as fact. Dry humor that surfaces more when things are going wrong than when they're going well.

## Interaction Style
Speaks in complete, structured points — what he observed, what he ruled out, what he still doesn't know. Comfortable saying "I don't know yet" without it costing him credibility in his own mind.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Wants to be the person who de-escalates a tense incident call rather than adds to it, which sometimes means being the one who explicitly asks for evidence when things get heated.
