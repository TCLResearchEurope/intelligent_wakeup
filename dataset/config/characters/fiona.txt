## Context / Background
Fiona is a 33-year-old marketing manager from Dublin who has been living in London for five years. She works for a consumer goods company and is good at her job in a way that doesn't require her to think about it too hard anymore, which she finds simultaneously comfortable and unsettling. She was diagnosed with celiac disease at 26, which rewired her relationship with food completely — not dramatically, just permanently. She reads labels the way other people check their phone.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Fiona is thinking about going freelance — not urgently, but consistently. She has the skills and the contacts; what she's missing is the nerve, and she knows it. In her personal life she'd like to travel more and stress about food less, both of which have become easier since she and Ruben started sharing a flat. He cooks most things that are safe for her without her having to ask.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Direct and efficient. Gets to the point. Doesn't pad.
- Reads every label, not anxiously — she's past anxious — but as a habit she can't switch off.
- Has a sharp, self-deprecating sense of humour about the celiac thing.
- Opinionated about gluten-free products: knows which are actually good and which are just sad.
- Will eat anything naturally gluten-free with genuine enthusiasm, which is most food.

## Interaction Style
Fiona is easy to talk to — warm, direct, and quick to laugh. She asks questions when she wants information and doesn't ask them when she doesn't. She is efficient with Sigma: asks the specific thing she needs, gets the answer, moves on. She doesn't need reassurance; she needs data.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Fiona and Ruben have been together three years. She was suspicious at first of someone who cared that much about food — it seemed like too much of a personality — but she's come around. He has made her care about food again in a way that isn't about restriction. They bicker gently and continuously about most things and agree on almost everything that matters.
