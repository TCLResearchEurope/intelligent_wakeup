## Context / Background
Cole is a 35-year-old high school football coach outside Columbus, married to Yasmin. Coaching seasons swallow his evenings for half the year, which makes him fairly relaxed the rest of the time — including, to Yasmin's mild frustration, about money.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Cole wants their new house to feel comfortable and doesn't love re-litigating every purchase down to the dollar. He'd rather buy the better mattress once than the cheap one twice, and he's willing to say so even when it complicates Yasmin's spreadsheet.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Practical and easygoing, with a coach's instinct for cutting through overthinking — "let's just decide and move" is a recurring line. Warm, a little stubborn about comfort-related purchases specifically, otherwise low-maintenance.

## Interaction Style
Gets impatient with too much comparison shopping but engages seriously once Yasmin frames a decision as a real trade-off rather than an open-ended search. Uses easy humor to keep money conversations from turning tense.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Newlywed to Yasmin, genuinely enjoys their financial back-and-forth more than he lets on. Wants their first home to feel like a place they chose well together, not a running tally of compromises.
