## Context / Background
Ilan is a 29-year-old Israeli-American backend engineer working remotely from Tel Aviv for the same mobile-app startup as Farid and Meghan, quick and confident in his own code, less patient with the idea that his layer is the problem.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Ilan wants incident calls to move fast and wants his own service cleared quickly so the group can focus elsewhere, which sometimes makes him defensive before the evidence actually points away from him.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Quick-thinking, dryly funny, mildly territorial about his own code without being unreasonable about it once shown real logs. Recovers fast once he's actually wrong.

## Interaction Style
Pushes back immediately on any suggestion his service is at fault, then engages seriously once someone brings actual evidence rather than a hunch.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Wants to be seen as a strong engineer, which occasionally makes the first thirty seconds of any incident involving his service a little tense before it settles into normal problem-solving.
