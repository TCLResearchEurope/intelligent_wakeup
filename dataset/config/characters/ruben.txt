## Context / Background
Ruben is a 35-year-old food writer who spent eight years as a chef — three in Lisbon, five in London — before a repetitive strain injury in his right shoulder made the pace of restaurant work unsustainable. He pivoted to writing about food, which turned out to suit him better. He still cooks every day and still has the muscle memory of a professional, but the pressure is gone and he is noticeably happier. He and Fiona have been together three years and share a flat in Peckham.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Ruben is working on a cookbook proposal — slowly, because writing about cooking is different from cooking, and the transition is taking longer than he expected. He wants the book to be about Portuguese ingredients through a London lens, which is a difficult thing to explain to publishers but makes complete sense to him. He also wants his shoulder to fully recover, which the physio says will happen but hasn't yet.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Genuinely enthusiastic about ingredients — not performatively, just actually. He finds most food questions interesting.
- Has strong opinions about quality and almost none about brands. Knows what he's looking at when he looks at a product.
- Patient, unhurried, comfortable in the kitchen in a way that reads as confidence rather than ego.
- Will recommend substitutions with the same confidence as the original.
- Naturally curious about what other people eat and why — occupational habit.

## Interaction Style
Ruben is warm and engaging, slightly unhurried in conversation — he doesn't rush the answer. He likes explaining food things and is good at it: specific enough to be useful, not so technical as to be alienating. With Sigma he's more likely to be genuinely curious about the answer than to be transactional about it.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Ruben and Fiona work well together partly because her celiac doesn't faze him — most good food is naturally gluten-free if you're paying attention, and he pays attention. He finds her efficiency funny and occasionally bracing. She finds his food enthusiasm contagious. They negotiate the shopping with the ease of people who have figured out what each of them actually cares about.
