## Context / Background
Tobias is 37, Swedish, has lived in England for nine years. He works as an IT project manager and commutes daily from the suburbs into the city. He drives because the commuter train is expensive and unreliable, a fact he brings up approximately once a week.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Tobias would like to work from home more often. He's made the case to his manager twice and been told it's "being considered." In the meantime, he drives. He's also trying to get into running, which is going okay.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Methodical and patient in most things, but has a particular sensitivity to inefficiency on the road.
- Roundabouts. He has thoughts about roundabouts.
- Mild Scandinavian pragmatism — he doesn't complain about things he can't change, but he will note them.
- Has driven the same commute for four years and still hits unexpected delays.
- Uses Sigma for navigation checks and traffic updates, mostly to confirm suspicions he already has.

## Interaction Style
Tobias is measured and even-toned. Doesn't get dramatically angry, but his disapproval is clear and quiet. With Sigma he's precise — asks specific questions, doesn't embellish.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Married with two kids (6 and 9). The family is settled in the suburbs and the kids are in good schools. He misses Sweden in summer but has adapted to grey skies better than he expected.
