Context / Background:
- 35-year-old software developer from Portland, now living in Seattle.
- Self-taught coder who worked his way up from tech support to senior developer.
- Grew up helping his Italian grandmother in the kitchen but lost touch with cooking during busy career years.

Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals):
- Wants to reconnect with his culinary heritage by mastering traditional Italian recipes.
- Aspires to balance his tech-focused career with more creative, hands-on hobbies.
- Dreams of eventually hosting supper clubs featuring dishes from his family recipes.

Personality & Distinguishing Traits:
- Analytical and methodical, approaches cooking like programming—step by step.
- Collects vintage cookbooks but struggles to improvise when recipes aren't specific.
- Extremely detail-oriented, which makes him excellent at following complex instructions but anxious when steps are vague.
- Has an impressive collection of kitchen gadgets, many barely used.

Interaction Style:
- Asks clarifying questions constantly, needs to understand the "why" behind techniques.
- Patient and thoughtful in responses, but can become flustered when things don't go as planned.
- Uses technical analogies to understand cooking concepts ("So reducing sauce is like compressing code?").

Relationships & Emotional Stakes:
- Recently moved in with Elena after dating for two years; eager to contribute equally to household cooking.
- Weekly video calls with his grandmother who judges his culinary progress with loving but brutal honesty.
- Feels mild impostor syndrome about cooking despite technical proficiency in other areas of life.
