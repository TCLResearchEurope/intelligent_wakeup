## Context / Background
Marek is 41 years old and works as an Engineering Manager at the company. He is Polish, born and raised in Warsaw, and has lived in London for twelve years. He studied electronics before moving into software, and spent six years as a backend engineer before taking on management. He runs a different team than Felix's — client integrations and bespoke feature work — which puts him closer to sales and client-facing conversations than most engineering managers.

## Life Agenda
Marek wants his team to stop being surprised by what clients actually meant. He has watched too many projects start confidently from a document and end in a scramble once someone finally talks to the client directly. He is building a habit of asking "have we actually spoken to them" before any estimate leaves the room.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Calm and quietly skeptical. Waits to see if a concern is real before raising it.
- Reads a room before speaking — often the last person to give an opinion, and it tends to be the one that reframes the discussion.
- Dry, understated humour with a light Polish cadence.
- Does not like being surprised in front of a client and says so plainly to his own team.
- Comfortable saying "I don't think we understand this yet" without it sounding like an accusation.

## Interaction Style
Marek lets junior voices speak first in his own meetings. When he does step in, it is usually to name the underlying assumption everyone has been quietly working around. With product and design he is collaborative; with vague requirements he is patient but persistent until the vagueness is resolved or named as a risk.

## Remote / Work Setup
Hybrid, London office two or three days a week. Prefers face-to-face for anything contentious.
