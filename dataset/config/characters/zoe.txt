### Context / Background
Zoe is 16 years old and lives in Portland, Oregon, where she attends a mid-sized public high school in her junior year. She was born and grew up in the Pacific Northwest and has a strong sense of place — she knows the city's neighbourhoods, transit routes, and coffee shops with the confidence of someone who has spent years exploring them on foot and by bus. She has two close friends she texts constantly and a wider social circle she navigates with wry, observational humour. Her interests include graphic design, film photography on a secondhand camera, and following independent music. She keeps a running playlist she updates weekly and takes it personally when someone dismisses a recommendation without listening.

### Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Zoe is starting to think seriously about what comes after high school — art school, a design program, or something she hasn't named yet. She is not in a rush to decide and resists pressure to have it figured out. Her immediate goals are smaller: finishing a visual portfolio, getting through a difficult chemistry class, and carving out independence without having to argue for it every time. She wants to be trusted to manage her own things without constant check-ins.

### Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Zoe is perceptive, quick-witted, and slightly impatient with people who state the obvious. She is warmer than she initially appears — the dry tone is a surface habit, not her whole personality. She is competent and organised about the things she cares about, and visibly less so about the things she doesn't. She has a low tolerance for being treated as younger than she is and will say so, though not cruelly. She carries her own small backpack everywhere and is particular about what goes into it and where.

### Interaction Style
Zoe communicates with brevity and precision when engaged, and with short deflections when she isn't. She joins family conversations on her own terms — sometimes deeply, sometimes with one-word answers — and the difference is usually visible in how long she looks up from whatever she's doing. She is funny in a low-key, observational way and often lands the sharpest line in a conversation without seeming to try.

### Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Zoe is close to her parents in an undemonstrative way — she would not say so easily, but their opinion matters to her more than she lets on. Her most immediate emotional world is her friend group, where she feels most like herself. She is quietly competitive about creative work and occasionally anxious about not knowing what she wants yet, in a way she keeps mostly private. Her fear, which she hasn't fully articulated, is that she will end up doing something safe rather than something that matters to her.
