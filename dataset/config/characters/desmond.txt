## Context / Background
Desmond is a 45-year-old semi-retired contractor in Atlanta, British-Jamaican by upbringing before settling in the US, Renee's partner for the past three years. He still takes the occasional job but mostly consults, which leaves him with strong, practical opinions about home projects and household spending.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Desmond wants to avoid buying things they'll regret, especially now that he and Renee are sharing more expenses. He trusts hands-on experience over online reviews, which sometimes puts him at odds with Renee's more research-driven approach.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Practical, unhurried, mildly skeptical of anything that sounds too polished online. Warm underneath a gruff first impression. Doesn't enjoy conflict but won't just go along with something he thinks is a bad call.

## Interaction Style
Speaks plainly, prefers a concrete example over an abstract argument. Willing to be convinced but wants to see the actual reasoning, not just an assertion.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Settling into a genuinely good partnership with Renee, still working out their shared decision-making style around money and purchases. Wants to be a good, reliable partner without becoming the household's default skeptic-in-chief.
