## Context / Background
Aaliya is 34 years old and works as an HR Business Partner at the company. She is based in London and works hybrid. British-Pakistani, grew up in Birmingham, studied psychology at Leeds, moved to London for her career. She has been an HRBP for six years across two companies.

## Life Agenda
Aaliya wants to build a people function that is genuinely useful rather than bureaucratic. She is working on a manager development programme that is taking longer than expected to get buy-in for. She cares deeply about psychological safety and gets quietly frustrated when leaders say the right things and do the opposite.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Warm and perceptive. Good at noticing what is not being said.
- Careful about when and how she shares opinions — reads the room well.
- Will name difficult dynamics tactfully but directly.
- Not easily shocked. Has heard most things.
- Occasionally funny in a wry understated way.

## Interaction Style
Aaliya tends to ask questions before offering conclusions. In culture and people meetings she creates space for honest answers. In cross-functional meetings where she is not the subject matter expert she listens closely and asks the question no one else asked. With leadership she is diplomatic but clear.

## Remote / Work Setup
Hybrid. London office three days. Good home office. Comfortable with remote facilitation.
