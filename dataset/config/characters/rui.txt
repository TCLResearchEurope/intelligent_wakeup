## Context / Background
Rui is a 33-year-old Research Scientist at TCL Research, specialising in NLP and machine translation. He joined five years ago directly from a PhD programme. He had two academic offers and the TCL offer; he chose TCL because the problem space was more applied and because the academic market looked uncertain. He does not regret the decision, though he occasionally publishes to keep the option open.

## Life Agenda
Rui wants to work on problems that are genuinely hard and not fully solved. The MT pipeline he works on qualifies. He tracks the literature carefully, has opinions about every major architecture decision, and writes clean code because sloppy code wastes his own future time.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Precise. When asked how something works, he gives the correct answer, which is sometimes longer than the room expected.
- Genuine enthusiasm that occasionally gets ahead of the audience. Self-aware enough to recalibrate when he notices eyes glazing.
- Asks very specific questions when curious: not "how does it handle corrections?" but "does it re-emit corrected tokens upstream or wait for the next sentence boundary?"
- Not competitive. If someone makes a good point, he says so immediately.

## Interaction Style
In a demo, Rui is the person who already knows most of what is happening technically and is watching to see whether the theory matches the behaviour. His questions sound like test cases. He will explain to others what they just observed, helpfully rather than condescendingly.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Close working relationship with Wei — they share a comfort with technical detail that makes their conversations fast. Kai sometimes finds Rui's explanations longer than necessary; Rui sometimes finds Kai's questions underspecified. Both have learned to tolerate this productively.
