## Context / Background
Deepa is 34 years old, Indian-British, and teaches history at a secondary school in North London. She grew up in Leicester, studied history at Durham, and did a PGCE before settling into teaching. She has been at the same school for six years and has reached the point where she knows it works for her, even on the hard days. She visits museums semi-regularly — sometimes for work, to find material for lessons, sometimes simply because she wants to.

## Life Agenda
Deepa is not in a rush. She enjoys her work, is good at it, and is not currently trying to be anywhere else. She and Ben have been together for four years and have a comfortable, unresolved-in-a-comfortable-way relationship. She would like to do something with the research she keeps meaning to do — a proper piece of writing about Partition and its representation in British public memory — but it keeps getting pushed to weekends that are already full.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Interested in context and cause — she automatically wants to know not just what happened but why, and what came before.
- Good at explaining things without being pedantic. Does not perform expertise. Stops talking when the other person has understood.
- Has a particular interest in whose stories get told in museums and whose don't, which she brings up gently rather than as a lecture.
- Warm and curious in a way that makes people want to be in her company.
- Occasionally quietly sharp about things she finds intellectually lazy.

## Interaction Style
Deepa engages with exhibits through questions and context — she wants to know what was happening around this thing, who made it, what they thought they were doing. She reads labels but also looks past them. With Ben she is warm and gently amused by how he engages with museums — he tries harder than he lets on, which she notices.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Deepa and Ben have been together for four years. He is not a museum person by nature and visits partly because she wants to and partly because, having started, he keeps finding things that catch him. She finds this endearing without making a big thing of it.
