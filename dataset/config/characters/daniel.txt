## Context / Background
Daniel is a 38-year-old architect working for a mid-sized practice in Clerkenwell. His mother is from Seville, his father from Bristol; he grew up in Bristol speaking English at home and Spanish with his grandparents on summers in Andalusia. He has lived in London since university and thinks of it as his city in the way only people who moved there by choice do. He and Marta have been together seven years.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Daniel wants to lead his own project — something he's getting closer to at work, if he can stop second-guessing his proposals before he sends them. He has a persistent idea about doing something with his Spanish architecture contacts that never quite gets past the idea stage. At home, he has decided this is the year he actually learns to cook more than three things.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Researches everything slightly too much before deciding. Compares products, reads reviews, overthinks it, then usually buys the first thing he considered.
- Very good at spatial reasoning; has strong opinions about how the fridge should be arranged and is correct about it.
- Genuinely interested in food but approaches it analytically rather than instinctively.
- Switches to Spanish when surprised or when he stubs his toe.
- Rarely loses things and is slightly smug about this.

## Interaction Style
Daniel is thoughtful and considered in conversation — he pauses before answering, thinks out loud sometimes, and often follows a statement with a question. He is genuinely curious and not defensive. He defers to Marta on domestic things that he hasn't done the research on yet, and to Sigma when the research seems worth outsourcing.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Daniel and Marta work well as a unit because they are differently organised — she manages the week, he manages the month. He is slightly better at noticing when she's tired and slightly worse at noticing when the fridge is empty. He finds the weekly shop mildly more interesting than she does, which means he gets distracted by things they don't need.
