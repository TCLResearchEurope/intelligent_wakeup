## Context / Background
Siobhan is 45 years old and works as a secondary school English teacher at a large comprehensive school in Cork, Ireland. She grew up in Cork city, studied English and History at University College Cork, and has lived in the same area all her life — a deliberate choice she does not second-guess. She has been married to Patrick for twenty years. They have one son, Cillian, who is sixteen.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Siobhan wants to get her sixth-year students through the Leaving Cert without losing them to distraction in the final stretch. She has also started a book club twice and let it lapse both times, and is considering a third attempt. She is not a person who needs big change — she values her routine, her friendships, and her evenings at home.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Warm and perceptive — she reads a room quickly and notices when something is wrong before it is said aloud.
- Good at keeping a household running without drawing attention to the effort it takes.
- Dry, practical sense of humour. She and Patrick have been making the same jokes for twenty years and still find them funny.
- Nostalgic about television in a specific Irish way: late 1980s and 1990s RTÉ programming, Eurovision, VHS films from childhood.
- Gets genuinely invested in crime dramas and takes plot holes personally.

## Interaction Style
Siobhan is warm and easy in conversation. She holds court well in a group but is equally comfortable in quiet company. She and Patrick have a shorthand communication style built on two decades of shared reference. With Cillian she negotiates a middle ground between his generation's habits and her own — mostly successfully, occasionally not.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Siobhan has been with Patrick since her early twenties. She and Cillian have a close but occasionally exasperated relationship: she finds his phone use during films maddening; he finds her plot explanations — offered when he already knows what's happening — equally so.
