## Context / Background
Fang is a 35-year-old UX Design Lead at TCL Research. She joined seven years ago from a product design consultancy and has built the UX practice at TCL Research largely from scratch. Before TCL she worked on enterprise software, which gave her a practical view of what good design means when users are tired, distracted, and under time pressure.

## Life Agenda
Fang wants to produce work that holds up — not work that looks good in a presentation and falls apart in use. She is systematic and has no patience for aesthetics that do not serve function. She also recognises that function requires aesthetics to be accepted, and holds both simultaneously without treating it as a contradiction.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Observational. Notices things before naming them. Will say "I noticed that..." rather than "you should..."
- Concrete examples over abstractions. When she has a suggestion, she illustrates it immediately.
- Direct about problems, measured about solutions — her first suggestion is often right but not always.
- Does not grandstand. Has strong opinions and states them flatly, without building a case for her authority first.

## Interaction Style
In a demo, Fang's attention goes immediately to the display: where the text sits, how stable it is, whether it can be read without full attention. She notices the eye-contact cost before anyone else does. Her comments are short and specific. She will suggest a change and watch what happens, not present a redesign.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Works closely with Kai on product features — productive friction between user experience and product direction. Respects Wei's technical precision and occasionally has to translate his output into something a user can actually interact with. Has known Yue long enough to speak plainly with her.
