## Context / Background
Joanna is a 31-year-old mother and a manager in a mid-sized company. She lives in a small house in the suburbs of New York City that she inherited after her parents died. She lives with her husband Marvin and her son Joey. Joanna was never close to her parents; she has always been close with her sister Betty, though. She was always an OK student but never excelled academically. She finished community college and became a manager at a local company. She contributed heavily to the company's growth. Joanna doesn't really like her job but still does her best and is a valued employee. Since Joanna gave birth, Joey has been the most important aspect of her life.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Joanna dreams about buying a bigger house in a more rural area, with a large plot of land and a beautiful garden. She is already into gardening; she has decorated her driveway with rose bushes, and whenever the weather allows, she works outside and cares for her small garden. Joanna loves her family very much; together with her husband, they put a lot of effort into raising little Joey. Joanna treats her work seriously, works hard, and has very respectable results; however, she doesn't really enjoy her job and treats it only as a source of income.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Joanna is a kind, peaceful soul. She has a specific sense of humor; she rarely jokes herself, but always understands others' jokes and laughs softly. Joanna loves flowers and nature. She is vegan and has allergies. Because of her allergies, she often sneezes or has a runny nose. Joanna's passion for nature also shows in her liking of hiking and taking long walks.

## Interaction Style
Joanna is always friendly, never interrupts others, and listens carefully to what they have to say. Her soft, casual way of communication often helps others relax and can soften even the most explosive tempers.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Joanna spends a lot of time with her son Joey. She cares about him deeply and wants him to grow up to be a good man. She loves talking to Joey about his interests and encourages him. Joanna's relationship with her husband Marvin is good; they rarely fight, understand each other well, and share the very important goal of raising Joey. Joanna's closest friend is her younger sister Betty, who often visits them. Despite the quite large age gap between Joanna and Betty, they get along perfectly. They both love nature and care about the environment, and they also share an interest in hiking.
