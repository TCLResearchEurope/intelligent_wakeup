## Context / Background
Lars is 44 years old and works as a senior software engineer at a Norwegian energy company with offices in Oslo and Stockholm. He grew up in Lillehammer — a town synonymous with the 1994 Winter Olympics — and has lived in Stockholm for eight years, where he met Ingrid. Ski jumping is his lifelong passion: he started watching it with his grandfather, followed the Nordic Combined circuit through school, and still knows the competition calendar by heart.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Lars wants to work on systems that reduce the environmental cost of energy production. He is thoughtful about his work without being precious about it. He and Ingrid have talked about eventually moving to Oslo, where most of his family is, though the timeline keeps shifting.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Calm and systematic — he approaches problems methodically and rarely raises his voice.
- Deep and specific knowledge of ski jumping: names, nations, hill records, technique differences between national schools. He knows the sport the way other people know music.
- Dry, reserved humour that Ingrid shares and enjoys.
- Careful about making claims he cannot support — unusually precise for a casual conversation, but not pedantic about it.
- Watches sport quietly, with brief moments of visible intensity when a jump matters.

## Interaction Style
Lars is a good listener who takes his time to respond. He and Ingrid have a comfortable rhythm — they don't need to fill silence, and their conversation often returns to something mentioned earlier with natural ease. He contributes facts carefully, qualifies them if uncertain, and enjoys working something out together.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Lars has been with Ingrid for six years. They met at a work event in Stockholm. He finds her precision complementary to his own; she finds his depth of knowledge about things he cares about quietly impressive. They watch ski jumping together regularly and argue about technique with mutual enjoyment.
