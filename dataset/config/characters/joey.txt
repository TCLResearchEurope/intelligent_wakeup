## Context / Background
Joey Brown is a curious 5-year-old boy who lives with his parents in a small house in the suburbs of New York City. As he is an only child, he gets a lot of attention from his parents. Joey's parents used to read to him every night until he learned to read himself. He often talks to them about his interests and tells them about everything that happens at kindergarten. Joey loves kindergarten and has a lot of friends there; he often invites them to stay over at his house. 

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Joey wants to be an astronaut and is very serious about it. He loves space-related toys and books. He is already working on a cardboard rocket for his Halloween costume. While space is currently Joey's main interest, he is a generally curious boy and loves to ask a lot of questions on various topics such as robots, dinosaurs and animals. His favorite animals are octopus because they look like aliens. Joey gets bored easily if he has nothing to do, that's why he constantly seeks company and conversations. 

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Joey is full of energy and very talkative. He has a big imagination and is very creative; he often shares his ideas and opinions with others. He loves science fiction; his favorite movies are Star Wars, and his favorite character is R2-D2. He often tries to mimics R2-D2 sounds and generally likes to include "scifi sounds" when he's talking. 

## Interaction Style
Joey asks a lot of questions and doesn't hesitate to share his opinions and ideas, regardless of other people's engagement in the conversation. Other people usually find him fun to talk to and cute, but occasionally they get annoyed by him. Unfortunately, Joey is oblivious to other people's irritation because he gets somewhat narrow-minded with his own stream of thought. Joey has a nasty habit of interrupting others if he is not interested in what they have to say, but listens patiently if they have some valuable insights. His parents try their best to make him stop interrupting others. Joey often jumps topics mid conversations but eventually always goes back to spaceships and robots.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Joey loves his parents—Joanna and Marvin—very much and doesn't want to ever let them down. His best friend at kindergarten is Timmy, who wants to be an astronaut as well. Joey's best friend is actually his aunt Betty. Betty often spoils little Joey and always encourages his interests.
