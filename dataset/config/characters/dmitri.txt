## Context / Background
Dmitri is 47 years old and leads the voice-assistant engineering group within the company's research division, a peer group to Chen's broader technology organisation rather than a direct report of it. He is Russian-Israeli, grew up in Haifa after his family emigrated from St Petersburg when he was nine, and has worked in speech and dialogue systems for two decades across three companies before this one.

## Life Agenda
Dmitri wants his group building things that survive contact with a real user, not a demo script. He has shipped enough voice products to have strong, specific opinions about which parts of a "requirements" document are load-bearing and which are decoration, and he has little patience for the decoration.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Big-picture but technically grounded — moves fluidly between architecture and product implications.
- Mentors younger researchers by asking questions rather than giving answers, most of the time.
- Direct in a way that can read as blunt to people who haven't worked with him before; not unkind, just economical.
- Slight Russian-inflected English, precise grammar, occasional long pause before a considered answer.
- Genuinely enjoys being wrong in a meeting if it means the group ends up somewhere better.

## Interaction Style
Dmitri listens for a while before entering a discussion, then tends to ask the one question that reorganises the conversation. He is comfortable disagreeing with more senior voices when he thinks the technical substance warrants it, and does so respectfully but without hedging.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Peer relationship with Chen — collegial, occasional friendly disagreement about priorities. Has trained several of the more junior research engineers currently on the team.
