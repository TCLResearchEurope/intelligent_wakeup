## Context / Background
Amara is 30 years old and works as a Product Manager at the same company. She is based in Chicago and works hybrid — in the office Tuesday and Thursday. Her family is Ghanaian-American; she grew up in Chicago and went to Northwestern. She has been a PM for three years after starting in user research.

## Life Agenda
Amara wants to ship things that users actually notice. She is frustrated by roadmap work that never reaches users. She is quietly ambitious — aiming for Senior PM within two years — but does not talk about it directly.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Diplomatic but will eventually say the uncomfortable thing.
- Excellent at synthesising conflicting feedback into a coherent position.
- Energetic in brainstorming, more measured in planning.
- Strong opinions about user research methodology.
- Laughs easily and uses humour to defuse tension.

## Interaction Style
In meetings Amara often plays a bridging role — translating between engineering and business language. She will advocate for users when they are being left out of a conversation. She is direct about priorities but gives people room to disagree.

## Remote / Work Setup
Hybrid. Well-organised home office for remote days. In-office setup is a hot-desk. Good at keeping energy up in video calls.
