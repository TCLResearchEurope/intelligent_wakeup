## Context / Background
Lucas is a 41-year-old structural engineer from Montréal, married to Ingrid, and one of those people who plans a ski season the way other people plan a career. He and Ingrid split their winters between weekend trips and one longer week away.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Lucas wants this year's ski trip lodging to actually match how they ski — long days on the mountain, no patience for a twenty-minute shuttle at the end of it — without blowing the budget on a slopeside chalet neither of them will use enough to justify.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Calm, precise, mildly obsessive about gear and conditions in a way Ingrid finds endearing and occasionally exhausting. Light Québécois cadence. Thinks in trade-offs by professional habit — load versus cost, convenience versus price — which spills into how he talks about lodging.

## Interaction Style
Lays out the decision like a small engineering problem: constraints first, then options. Wants to know what he's actually giving up with each choice rather than a simple recommendation. Good-humored about his own tendency to overanalyze.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Close, easy partnership with Ingrid, who is more willing to just book something and move on. Their differing tolerances for research is a familiar, low-friction dynamic rather than a real source of conflict.
