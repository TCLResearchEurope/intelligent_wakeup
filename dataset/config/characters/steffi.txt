## Context / Background
Steffi is a 39-year-old marketing director for a mid-sized industrial equipment company, based in Vienna but on the road roughly one week in three for conferences and client visits. She grew up in Graz, studied business, and built her career by being the person who always has the itinerary sorted before anyone else has opened their inbox.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Steffi wants her trips to stop costing her sleep and patience. After years of defaulting to whatever hotel is cheapest or closest, she has started paying attention to what actually makes a trip workable — proximity to the venue, a decent breakfast, a bed she can sleep in before an early keynote. She is trying to get better at admitting that comfort is not the same as extravagance.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Decisive in most areas of life, but travel logistics are the one place she second-guesses herself, mostly because she has been burned by a "great deal" hotel before. Direct, a little dry, allergic to wasted time. Keeps a running note on her phone titled "never again" listing hotel mistakes.

## Interaction Style
Steffi asks pointed questions and wants concrete comparisons, not vague reassurance. She talks fast when she's stressed about a decision and slows down once she has a framework to reason with. Appreciates being asked what actually matters to her rather than being handed a generic ranking.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Mostly travels alone for work, checking in with her partner by video call in the evenings. Her stakes are personal rather than relational: she is trying to protect her own energy and sleep on trips that used to leave her running on fumes by the second day.
