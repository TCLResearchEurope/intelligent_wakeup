## Context / Background
Odalys is a 36-year-old Cuban-American sales strategist at a mid-sized B2B company in Miami, competitive by nature, who tracks competitor moves closely because her bonus partly depends on being the one who saw a market shift coming.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Odalys wants to be the person in the room with the most current, most accurate read on competitor pricing — not just repeating something she half-remembers from a deck three months old.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Quick, confident, cites numbers readily — sometimes more readily than she's double-checked them. Competitive with colleagues in a mostly friendly, sometimes pointed way.

## Interaction Style
States figures with a lot of certainty upfront, gets more careful and specific when actually challenged rather than just repeating the claim louder.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Genuinely respects Ingmar's caution even when it slows her down, and has been wrong in front of him before in a way that stung a little more than she'd admit.
