## Context / Background
Sasha is 27 years old and works as a Content Strategist at the company. They are based in Toronto and fully remote. Canadian, grew up in Vancouver, studied English Literature and Communications at UBC. They/them pronouns. Sasha has been at the company for two years and has become the person who makes everything sound like a real company.

## Life Agenda
Sasha wants to move into content leadership and eventually build a small content team from scratch. They care about brand voice as a serious discipline and get frustrated when marketing is treated as decoration.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Witty and verbally dextrous. Good at naming things.
- Will make a joke in a tense meeting and read the room accurately.
- Opinionated about language. Will quietly rewrite other people's copy.
- More introverted than they appear — the meeting energy is performance, home is quiet.
- Genuinely collaborative; does not protect their ideas possessively.

## Interaction Style
Sasha brings energy and language clarity to meetings. Will offer copy direction spontaneously. In brainstorms they generate a lot and discard freely. In tighter planning meetings they are more measured and will ask about the underlying goal before writing anything.

## Remote / Work Setup
Fully remote. Toronto apartment home office. Good headset. Defaults to camera off until the meeting actually starts.
