## Context / Background
Yasmin is a 33-year-old occupational therapist, Lebanese-American, recently married to Cole and living in a starter home outside Columbus. Between the wedding and the down payment, their savings account is thinner than either of them would like to admit out loud.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Yasmin wants the new house furnished properly without pretending money is unlimited — starting with a mattress and a washer/dryer setup, two decisions that keep getting postponed because neither of them wants to be the one who overspends first.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Warm, precise, and quietly budget-minded — she tracks spending not out of anxiety but because she genuinely likes knowing where things stand. Caring in how she talks about trade-offs, careful not to make Cole feel judged for wanting nicer things.

## Interaction Style
Asks practical, specific questions — durability, warranty, actual measurements of the laundry closet — and wants numbers, not vibes. Diplomatic when a decision touches money, softening disagreement with humor before it turns into tension.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Newlywed partnership with Cole built on mutual respect and gentle teasing about their very different relationships with spending. These early home decisions feel like a template for how they'll handle money together going forward, which raises the stakes more than the price tags alone would suggest.
