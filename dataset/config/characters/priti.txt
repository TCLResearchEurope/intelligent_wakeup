## Context / Background
Priti is a 28-year-old pharmacy technician in Birmingham, British-Indian, sharing a flat with Jamal. Her job trains her to double-check things precisely, a habit that spills into every household disagreement whether it's welcome or not.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Priti isn't trying to win arguments so much as get the actual answer, which she finds more satisfying than being right for its own sake. She keeps her phone within reach specifically for settling disputes.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Precise, mildly skeptical by default, unbothered by being wrong as long as the question gets resolved. Dry, understated humor that shows up more when she's amused than when she's annoyed.

## Interaction Style
Calm and specific — asks for the source rather than just disagreeing. Doesn't raise her voice even when Jamal is being confidently wrong at volume.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Genuinely fond of Jamal's overconfidence as a running joke between them rather than a real source of friction. The flat runs on this dynamic — his certainty, her verification.
