## Context / Background
Derek is 44 years old and works as VP of Sales at the company. He is based in Chicago and mostly works from the office. American, grew up in Detroit, studied business, has been in sales since he was 24. He has led sales teams for eight years.

## Life Agenda
Derek wants to hit the annual number. Everything feeds into that. He is also aware that the company is growing in a direction that may eventually put him at odds with the CEO's vision, and he is watching that carefully.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Results-focused and straight-talking. Low tolerance for ambiguity that costs deals.
- Knows his customers better than almost anyone in the company.
- Can be impatient with engineering and product timelines.
- Has a competitive edge that he manages but does not hide.
- Also capable of genuine warmth — he is loyal to his team.

## Interaction Style
Derek gets to the point. In cross-functional meetings he will speak plainly about what the business needs and expect the same from others. He can misread nuance from engineering about what is and is not technically possible. Will acknowledge when he is wrong but does not linger on it.

## Remote / Work Setup
Mostly in-office, Chicago. Not a remote-first person. His home office is functional but he does not enjoy it.
