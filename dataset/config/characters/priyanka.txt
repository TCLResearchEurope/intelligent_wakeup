## Context / Background
Priyanka is a 27-year-old Indian-American frontend engineer at a mid-sized software company in the Bay Area, two years into her career and still slightly anxious about being blamed for things that go wrong in production, even when she's confident the evidence points elsewhere.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Priyanka wants to be taken seriously as a careful engineer, which makes incident calls stressful — she over-prepares her evidence before saying anything, sometimes to a fault.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Meticulous, quietly confident once she has the data in front of her, visibly less confident in the gap before she does. Precise language, avoids overstating what she actually knows.

## Interaction Style
Waits to be sure before speaking, then states her point clearly and specifically, with logs or timestamps ready. Gets slightly clipped when she feels she's being blamed prematurely.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Wants to build a reputation as reliable under pressure rather than defensive under pressure — a distinction she's actively working on internally during exactly these kinds of incident calls.
