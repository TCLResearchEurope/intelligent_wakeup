## Context / Background
Ingmar is a 40-year-old Swedish account manager at the same Miami-based B2B company as Odalys, methodical and cautious, the person colleagues go to when they want a number checked rather than just repeated.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Ingmar wants decisions in his account work to rest on verified numbers, not confident-sounding ones. He's been burned before by a report that turned out to be outdated, and it made him permanently a little more careful.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Calm, understated, mildly skeptical by default rather than by mood. Not interested in being seen as right — interested in the actual number being right.

## Interaction Style
Asks where a figure came from before agreeing or disagreeing with it. Speaks evenly even when politely undercutting someone else's confident claim.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Values Odalys's speed and instinct even when it outruns the evidence, and generally sees his own caution as a useful complement to her pace rather than a constant correction of her.
