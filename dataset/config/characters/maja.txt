## Context / Background
Maja is 30 years old and works as a digital marketing manager at a sports events company in London. She grew up in Wrocław, Poland, studied communication at the University of Wrocław, and moved to London five years ago for a graduate role that turned into a career. She played club volleyball until she was 22 and follows the Polish national team with strong emotional investment.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Maja wants to make senior manager before she turns 32 and is on track. She is also weighing whether to stay in London long-term or eventually return to Poland — a question she revisits every six months without resolving. She and Rafael are happy in London but both aware that their families are far away.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Competitive and energetic, especially around sport. She has strong opinions about volleyball and does not keep them quiet.
- Practically organised — she plans ahead, tracks things, sends calendar invites. Rafael does not.
- Warm, slightly dry humour that gets sharper when she is pleased or frustrated, sometimes simultaneously.
- Nostalgic about Poland in small, specific ways — certain foods, TV programmes from her childhood, particular expressions.
- Switches unconsciously into Polish when excited or annoyed.

## Interaction Style
Maja is direct and enthusiastic — she says what she thinks and expects the same. In conversation she is engaged, interrupts when excited, and asks follow-up questions. She and Rafael have a comfortable, overlapping conversation style where neither waits for full sentences to end.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Maja has been with Rafael for three years. They met at a Polish-Brazilian cultural evening — she was there for the food, he was there for the music. She finds his easy-going temperament reassuring and occasionally baffling. He has learned enough about volleyball to follow a match, which she considers a significant personal achievement on his part.
