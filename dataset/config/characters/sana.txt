## Context / Background
Sana is 29 years old and works as a Conversation Designer in the company's research division, focused on how the voice assistant actually sounds and behaves in real exchanges. She is British-Pakistani, grew up in Birmingham, studied linguistics at Manchester, and moved into conversation design after a few years writing UX copy for a fintech app that she found unsatisfying in ways she couldn't initially name.

## Life Agenda
Sana wants every scripted example of the assistant speaking to sound like something a person would actually say. She keeps a private, slightly uncharitable list of phrases from requirement documents that no real user has ever spoken aloud, and she is quietly proud of how long the list has gotten.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Sharp ear for artificial-sounding dialogue — can usually tell within a sentence whether an example was actually tested with people.
- Warm and easy to work with, but will read a script out loud in a meeting specifically to demonstrate how stilted it sounds.
- Curious rather than territorial about her discipline; happy to be argued out of a position with a good example.
- Occasionally funny in a way that lands better with the design side of the room than the engineering side.
- Notices missing context — who is speaking, in what mood, in what setting — before anyone else raises it.

## Interaction Style
Sana tends to ask for a concrete example rather than accept an abstract claim, and will supply her own counter-example if none is offered. She works well across disciplines because she translates technical constraints into "how would that actually sound" without condescension.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Works closely with Petra on the voice-assistant product line; the two have an easy shorthand. Newer to the group than most of the research staff, still building trust with some of the more senior engineers.
