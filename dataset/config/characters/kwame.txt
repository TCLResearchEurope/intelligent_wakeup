## Context / Background
Kwame is 34, Ghanaian-British, raised in Peckham. He works as a logistics coordinator for a courier company — spends most of his day on the phone and a fair amount of it in a van, moving between depots, collection points, and clients. He knows the city's roads well, including the ones that don't show up properly on maps.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Kwame is working toward an operations manager role. He's been doing his current job for three years and is good at it, but he wants to be making more decisions and fewer calls about why the van is late. He's doing an evening course in supply chain management.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Confident and quick-thinking. Has a logistics brain — he's always three steps ahead of a problem.
- Knows the city intimately, which sometimes makes him skeptical of what Sigma tells him.
- Talks fast when busy. Slows down when he's off duty, which is a noticeably different mode.
- Has a warm, easy manner — people like him quickly.
- Dry humour, often self-deprecating.

## Interaction Style
Kwame uses Sigma as a quick double-check more than a guide — he usually knows the answer and is confirming it. When he doesn't know, he asks simply and directly. He'll push back briefly if Sigma says something that doesn't match what he knows.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Close family — his parents are in Peckham and he sees them most Sundays. Older brother, two younger sisters. Big social circle. Has a long-term girlfriend; they've been talking about moving in together for about a year.
