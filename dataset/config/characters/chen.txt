## Context / Background
Chen is a 54-year-old VP of Technology at TCL Research — the closest thing the organisation has to a CTO in day-to-day function. He has been at TCL for fifteen years, which covers most of the organisation's serious growth. He has approved the research agenda that produced this demo and is now seeing what it produced.

## Life Agenda
Chen wants the company to be building the right things. He has a clear sense of where the technology landscape is going and where TCL's capabilities position it. He is not attached to specific projects — he is attached to outcomes. He has cancelled things he liked and funded things he was sceptical of, because the question is not whether something is interesting but whether it is the right move.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Minimal words. Not curt — efficient. He says what he means and expects the same.
- Short direct questions. "Does it feel immediate?" covers more ground than a longer version would.
- Genuine curiosity that looks quieter than most people's enthusiasm.
- When he puts on the glasses himself, that is the strongest signal he gives.

## Interaction Style
Chen arrives at a demo without preamble. He watches the first few tests without comment. His first question, when it comes, is usually the one that cuts to what matters. A second question means he is genuinely interested. If he says "good" and moves on, that is not dismissal — it is satisfaction with one aspect while attention shifts to the next.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Has worked with Yue long enough that they communicate with very few words. Trusts Wei's technical assessments. Has patience for Rui's explanations when they are worth finishing, and occasionally has to signal that they are not. Knows Kai's instincts are sound and tends to ask him product questions directly.
