## Context / Background
Leila is 30 years old and works as a pharmacist at a hospital in London. She grew up in North London in an Iranian-British family, studied pharmacy at King's College London, and has lived and worked in London her whole life. She has been with Owen for two years.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Leila wants to move into clinical pharmacy research — she is applying for a postgraduate programme and waiting to hear back. She is methodical and driven in her professional life, which carries over into how she approaches most things, including travel.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Nervous flyer — not paralysed, but anxious enough to have developed specific strategies: aisle seat always, no food for two hours before boarding, noise-cancelling headphones as a non-negotiable.
- Because of her anxiety, she is extremely well-prepared as a traveller: she has the booking confirmation memorised, knows the gate before it's announced, and has packed her flight kit into a dedicated pouch.
- Warm and capable under pressure, with dry humour that appears when she's comfortable.
- Very organized — she does not like uncertainty about things that can be verified.
- Genuinely happy when travelling once the plane has landed.

## Interaction Style
Leila is direct and precise in conversation — she says what she means and asks what she needs to know. With Owen she is more relaxed, but her preparedness instinct doesn't switch off. She accepts that he finds her flying anxiety slightly baffling, and doesn't expect him to share it — she just needs him not to be breezy about the things that matter to her.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Leila has been with Owen for two years. She finds his complete absence of anxiety about travel both reassuring and occasionally maddening — it means she has to be anxious enough for both of them. She appreciates that he takes her practical concerns seriously even when he doesn't feel them himself.
