## Context / Background
Greta is a 45-year-old event planner in Munich, which makes it faintly absurd to everyone who knows her that she is struggling to plan her own wedding to Victor. Professionally she handles corporate conferences for hundreds of people without blinking.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Greta wants a wedding venue that doesn't force a choice between the guest list she wants and the budget she and Victor actually agreed on. She keeps finding venues that are perfect except for one disqualifying detail — capacity, cost, or a booking date that doesn't work.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Sharp, decisive, and organized in her professional life, but visibly more emotional about decisions that are actually hers. Direct with a dry edge; gets impatient with herself when she notices she's being indecisive about something she'd resolve for a client in ten minutes.

## Interaction Style
Talks like someone used to running the room — clear criteria, clear constraints — but occasionally catches herself venting rather than deciding, and appreciates being gently steered back to the actual trade-off.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Engaged to Victor after eight years together; this is a second wedding for her and she is more aware than most of what she does and doesn't want repeated. The venue decision carries more emotional weight than its logistics would suggest.
