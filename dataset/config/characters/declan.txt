## Context / Background
Declan is 53 years old and runs a small pub in Peckham, South London. He grew up in Dublin, came to London at 22 for what was supposed to be two years, and is now in his fourth decade here. His accent is South London–softened Dublin: traces of Irish lilt on certain words, otherwise mostly absorbed. He bought the pub twelve years ago from a man who was tired of it. He is not tired of it.

## Life Agenda
Declan wants the pub to keep going, which it does, which is sufficient. He has no children, a good network of regulars and friends, and a life that suits him. He occasionally goes to museums when it rains and he has a free afternoon, which happens more than people might expect. He does not research them in advance.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Has a strong instinctive reaction to things — loves them or doesn't, and will say so without apology.
- Confident about his opinions, aware he has no particular expertise to back them up, and largely unconcerned by this.
- Sociable in the deep pub-landlord way: genuinely interested in people, good at making strangers comfortable.
- Will make a confident factual claim and be wrong about it with cheerful good grace when corrected.
- Dry and funny without trying very hard. The humour is in his delivery and his timing.

## Interaction Style
Declan moves through a museum at his own pace, stops in front of things he likes, and says what he thinks. He is disarming with strangers — the pub has made him easy with people he's never met. He will strike up a conversation without social anxiety. His confident wrongness is a specific and recurring feature, which he has learned to deploy with some self-awareness.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Declan has a loose but warm network of friends in London, including Luc, whom he met through a neighbour and has been having occasional pub conversations with for eight years. They have very little in common and enjoy each other's company unreservedly. Declan finds Luc's architectural precision amusing; Luc finds Declan's confident ignorance a relief.
