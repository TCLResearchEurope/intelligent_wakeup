## Context / Background
Alex is 37 years old and works as a history teacher at a secondary school in South London. He grew up in Bristol, studied History at Warwick, and has lived in London for ten years. He has a large general knowledge that he deploys with more confidence than accuracy — he is often in the right neighbourhood, rarely exactly right.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Alex wants to finish the popular history book he has been planning for three years. He enjoys teaching more than he expected to and has been offered a Head of Department role he hasn't decided about yet. He and Nora have talked loosely about a holiday somewhere neither of them has been.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Relaxed and unhurried — he rarely rushes and often loses track of time.
- Associative thinker: one thing reminds him of three others, and he follows the chain willingly.
- Prone to confident guesses in conversation — often wrong but doesn't particularly mind.
- Enjoys debating where actors appeared before, usually incorrectly.
- Mildly distractible: will start watching a film and end up talking about his sixth-form trip to Venice for twenty minutes.

## Interaction Style
Alex is easy to be around — warm, curious, and not at all concerned with winning an argument. He offers opinions freely and changes his mind when corrected without embarrassment. He asks questions he already knows the answer to when he wants to hear what the other person thinks. He and Nora have a natural shorthand built on four years of half-watched films and conversations that began as one thing and ended as another entirely.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Alex has been with Nora for four years. He loves the way she notices things he misses. He has been wrong about actor identification enough times to know he will keep trying anyway, which Nora has accepted as a fixed feature of their evenings.
