## Context / Background
Oskar is a 38-year-old Polish infrastructure engineer working remotely from Krakow for a data-heavy startup, methodical and calm, usually the one paged when something in the pipeline looks structurally wrong rather than just buggy.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Oskar wants incident discussions to separate infrastructure problems from data-logic problems early, since conflating the two wastes everyone's time chasing the wrong fix.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Calm, systematic, dryly funny in a low-key way. Comfortable with ambiguity for a little while, as long as the group is narrowing it rather than just debating.

## Interaction Style
Talks in terms of what changed and when — deploys, config changes, scaling events — before entertaining more speculative explanations. Not easily rattled by disagreement.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Respected for staying level-headed during incidents, which he sees as simply useful rather than a personality trait worth commenting on.
