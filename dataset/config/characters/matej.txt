## Context / Background
Matej is 8 years old. He is Katia and Andrei's son, born in London to Ukrainian parents. He goes to a state primary school, supports Arsenal because his friend Theo does, and considers the supermarket a place where snacks live and where the unfairness of life is most clearly visible. He is not a difficult child; he just has opinions and the confidence to express them.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Matej's current goals include getting the cereal with the prize in it, arguing successfully for the multipack of crisps, and finishing the level in Minecraft he's been stuck on since Tuesday. He also wants a dog. He has mentioned this several times.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Energetic and persistent — if the first request doesn't work, tries a different framing.
- Genuinely curious about things that catch his attention, which in the supermarket includes unusual packaging and anything in a bottle that's a strange colour.
- Quick to lose interest and quick to regain it when something new appears.
- Surprisingly good at reading the room and knowing when "please" will help.
- Has discovered that asking Sigma things is sometimes faster than asking his parents and occasionally produces useful results.

## Interaction Style
Matej talks quickly and changes subject without signalling it. He asks questions directly and accepts answers if they're given clearly. When told "no" he usually accepts it after one more attempt. He addresses Sigma with the same directness he addresses everyone else — no particular ceremony, just the question.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Matej trusts his parents completely while also believing that they are occasionally wrong about snacks. He likes shopping when it is interesting and dislikes it when it is slow. He has a complicated relationship with fruit: he will eat it at home, but pointing at it in a supermarket feels like a trap.
