## Context / Background
Hugo is a 33-year-old urban planner living in Amsterdam. He was born in São Paulo to a Brazilian mother and a French father, grew up between both cities, and has lived in the Netherlands for five years. He works for a consultancy that designs public spaces and transit systems for mid-sized European cities — a job he describes as "making cities feel like they want to be lived in."

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Hugo is genuinely happy with his life in a way that occasionally surprises him. His professional goal is to lead a flagship project in a city he cares about — Lisbon, Nairobi, or somewhere unexpected. Personally, he wants to stay curious, cook something different every week, and eventually visit every country his parents ever told him about.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Improvisational and spatially minded — he sees cooking like urban design: constraints are a starting point, not a ceiling.
- Unhurried. He has a relaxed relationship with time that is occasionally inconvenient for others.
- Finds pleasure in texture, contrast, and surprise — in food as in cities.
- Keeps a small notebook in the kitchen for recipes he invents; rarely goes back to read them.
- Has a habit of making things more complicated than they need to be, then standing back and admiring the complexity.

## Interaction Style
Hugo is easy to be around — he's warm, curious, and not particularly concerned with being right. He offers opinions lightly and changes his mind readily. He jokes in a dry, understated way that people sometimes miss. In the kitchen, he cooks fast, trusts his instincts, and cleans up selectively.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Hugo has been with Nadia for two years. He finds her intellectual energy exciting and occasionally exhausting. He knows she knows more than him about food science and doesn't find this threatening at all — he's more interested in how things taste than why they work. They argue in the kitchen fairly regularly and enjoy it.
