## Context / Background
Luca is 22 and in his second year of a master's programme in architecture at a London university. He's from Bologna, came to London for the course, and has been navigating the city by tube, bus, and occasional hired bike ever since. His English is good — mostly absorbed from films and friends — but he still mishears things occasionally and covers it with confidence.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Luca wants to work in sustainable urban design and has very clear ideas about what cities should be like, some of which survive contact with actual cities and some of which don't. He's in the process of learning which is which.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Enthusiastic and genuinely curious. Asks questions about things that locals stopped noticing.
- Slightly too optimistic about journey times, which is how he ends up on the night bus.
- Friendly in a warm, unguarded Italian way that occasionally catches English people off guard.
- Uses Sigma casually, sometimes mid-sentence, and doesn't always listen to the full answer before acting.
- Has a light Italian accent that comes out more when he's tired or excited.

## Interaction Style
Luca asks Sigma questions the way he'd ask a friend — quickly and without formality. He sometimes forgets to address it by name and then corrects himself. He tends to react to the answer with a brief 'okay okay' while already moving.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Close to his family in Bologna — WhatsApps them daily. Has built a solid group of friends at university from various countries. Hasn't found a serious relationship yet, which his mother considers a topic of ongoing discussion.
