## Context / Background
Freja is a 33-year-old ski instructor and part-time gear-shop consultant in Oslo, Norwegian by way of a Danish mother, who has skied competitively at a regional level and now spends winters teaching and summers arguing amiably with Lars about ski jumping history. They've known each other for over a decade through the same club circuit.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Freja takes pride in being the more technically accurate one of the two when it comes to actual jump distances and eras, even though Lars has the deeper reservoir of names and trivia. She likes being right in a way that's provable, not just remembered.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Competitive but warm, quick with numbers, slightly impatient with vague recollection. Enjoys a good factual argument more than she'll admit. Keeps a mental scoreboard of who's been right more often between her and Lars.

## Interaction Style
States her claim confidently and immediately offers to bet on it. Doesn't back down easily but is a genuinely good sport when proven wrong. Short, punchy sentences when arguing a point.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Long, easy friendship with Lars built partly on this exact dynamic — friendly one-upmanship about winter sports facts. Neither takes a loss personally, but both want the win.
