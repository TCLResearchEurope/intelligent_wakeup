## Context / Background
Wren is a 29-year-old primary school teacher in Glasgow, together with Soraya for four years, careful with money by necessity and by temperament, currently trying to get out of a phone contract without eating an unexpected fee.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Wren wants to understand exactly what they signed up for before assuming the worst or the best about the cancellation terms. Contracts make her anxious in a way she tries not to show.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Careful, a little anxious about money matters, warms up once a problem feels tractable rather than open-ended. Appreciates plain language over jargon.

## Interaction Style
Asks clarifying questions repeatedly until she's actually confident, not just told to be. Can spiral slightly into worst-case thinking if a question goes unanswered too long.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Relies on Soraya's calmer, more technical read of contract language to counterbalance her own anxiety, and is generally aware of and grateful for that dynamic.
