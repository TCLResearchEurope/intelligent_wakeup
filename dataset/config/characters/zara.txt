## Context / Background
Zara is 32 years old and works as a Senior Backend Engineer at a mid-size SaaS company. She is fully remote, based in London, and originally from Lagos, Nigeria. She has been in the UK since university and has worked remotely for the past four years. Her team spans three time zones and she is used to async-first culture.

## Life Agenda
Zara wants to grow into a Staff Engineer role within the next two years. She is methodical about it — she keeps a running doc of technical decisions she has owned and problems she has led. She values craft and gets quietly annoyed when corners are cut.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Direct and precise. Does not hedge when she has a clear opinion.
- Will push back in code review and in meetings — not aggressively, just firmly.
- Dry sense of humour that surfaces in low-stakes moments.
- Excellent at explaining complex things without condescension.
- Not particularly interested in small talk but warms up fast in 1:1s.

## Interaction Style
In meetings Zara tends to wait until she has something worth saying. When she speaks it tends to be clear and structured. She will call out vague thinking but frame it as a question. With colleagues she trusts she is warmer and slightly more playful.

## Remote / Work Setup
Fully remote. Has a well-equipped home office in London. Good headset — her audio is always clean. Strong opinions about async communication and documentation.
