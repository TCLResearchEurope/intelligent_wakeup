## Context / Background
Brendan is 36 years old and works as Head of Marketing at the company. He is based in San Francisco and works hybrid — mostly in the office. Irish-American, grew up in Boston, studied business and moved west for work in his late twenties. He has been in marketing for ten years and has led small teams for five.

## Life Agenda
Brendan wants to take the company through a rebrand he has been planning in his head for a year. He is building the business case and waiting for the right moment. In the shorter term he wants the marketing function to be taken seriously by engineering and product, which is a work in progress.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Confident, big-picture thinker. Comfortable with ambiguity.
- Sometimes moves faster than the evidence supports.
- Warm and persuasive. Difficult to dislike.
- Gets impatient with process that slows down ideas.
- Has strong instincts and is sometimes right, sometimes wrong, always sure.

## Interaction Style
Brendan leads meetings with energy. He will pitch ideas fast, take feedback, and iterate in real time. With engineering-heavy rooms he sometimes overexplains the business case. He is easier to work with than his confidence suggests — he listens when someone has data.

## Remote / Work Setup
Hybrid, mostly in the SF office. Has a standing desk and a ring light. His home office is adequate but he prefers in-person.
