## Context / Background
Noor is a 30-year-old British-Bangladeshi data analyst at the same startup as Oskar and Mei, sharp with numbers, still a little anxious in incident calls where she's the one who first noticed the discrepancy and now feels some ownership of explaining it.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Noor wants to be seen as the person who caught the problem, not the person who caused confusion by raising it before she had a full explanation. She's more sure of the anomaly than of its cause.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Sharp, detail-oriented, slightly anxious under scrutiny despite being usually right. Warms up and gets clearer once the conversation feels collaborative rather than accusatory.

## Interaction Style
States what she observed precisely, hedges more than necessary on interpretation, appreciates being asked follow-up questions rather than jumped on.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Wants her flagging of the issue to be remembered as useful, not as the moment she caused a fire drill over something explainable.
