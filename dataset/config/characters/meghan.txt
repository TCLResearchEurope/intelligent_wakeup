## Context / Background
Meghan is a 29-year-old Irish-American QA engineer working remotely for the same startup as Farid, based in Chicago. She spends her working life trying to reproduce bugs precisely, which gives her a low tolerance for causal claims nobody has actually tested.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Meghan wants incident retros to end with an actual reproduced cause, not just a plausible-sounding story everyone agreed to stop arguing about. She'll keep digging a beat longer than is comfortable if the evidence doesn't add up yet.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Detail-oriented, patient, quietly relentless once she's decided something doesn't check out. Not combative — just unwilling to let an unverified claim pass without comment.

## Interaction Style
Asks precise clarifying questions, references specific test cases or logs rather than general impressions. Stays even-toned even when pointing out a flaw in someone else's theory.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Respected by the team for catching things others miss, occasionally finds herself the one person still asking questions after everyone else wants to move on.
