## Context / Background
Tessa is a 31-year-old marketing coordinator in Bristol, the older of two siblings, who has appointed herself the unofficial keeper of family history — recipes, dates, who-said-what at which Christmas. Her brother Marco finds this both useful and mildly infuriating.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Tessa wants the family's shared history to be remembered accurately, not just the version that's most flattering or most often repeated. She's the one who'd actually go digging through old photos or messages to check.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Organized, slightly bossy in a way she's aware of, genuinely loves a good factual rabbit hole. Protective of family lore but not precious about being wrong once shown evidence.

## Interaction Style
States things with more confidence than the evidence always supports, then softens when actually challenged. Enjoys being right but recovers well when she isn't.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Close, teasing relationship with Marco — lots of old sibling scorekeeping running underneath ordinary arguments. Underneath the bickering, she genuinely wants their shared history to survive accurately for their own kids one day.
