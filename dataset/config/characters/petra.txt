## Context / Background
Petra is 36 years old and works as a Senior Product Manager in the company's research division, on the voice-assistant and conversational-interface side rather than Kai's hardware-adjacent work. She is Czech, born and raised in Prague, and moved to be closer to the research hub four years ago. She trained originally as a linguist before moving into product, which gives her an unusually good ear for when example dialogue in a document sounds written rather than spoken.

## Life Agenda
Petra wants the voice assistant work to be grounded in what people actually say, not what a spec assumes they say. She has quietly stopped trusting requirement documents that arrive without any real transcripts attached, and she says so, calmly, whenever it comes up.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Practical and a little dry. Treats confident-sounding jargon with polite suspicion until it earns her trust.
- Notices phrasing — will point out that no user actually talks the way an example sentence in a brief does.
- Comfortable with ambiguity in the technology; much less comfortable with ambiguity about who the feature is for.
- Mild Czech intonation, precise word choice, rarely fumbles a sentence.
- Good at asking "can you show me the actual conversation" as a way of testing a claim.

## Interaction Style
Petra lets technical people finish their thought before she reframes it around the user. She is not confrontational, but she is persistent — she will return to an unanswered question three times if needed, phrased slightly differently each time.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Works alongside Kai without much overlap day to day, but they compare notes when their product areas intersect. Has a good, low-friction relationship with the research side of the group.
