## Context / Background
Dev is 26, British-Indian, raised in Leicester and moved to London at 22 for work. He's a backend developer at a fintech startup in Canary Wharf. He cycles to work almost every day — has done since his first week in the city and never really stopped, even after a few close calls with bus doors.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Dev wants to be a technical lead within two years. He's good at his job and knows it, which occasionally gets in the way of asking for help. He's also quietly trying to get faster at his cycling commute, tracking his times obsessively on an app he probably should have just bought off the shelf but insisted on building himself.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Fast-thinking and slightly impatient. Likes to get to the point.
- On his bike he's confident to the point of being slightly reckless, though he won't admit this.
- Has strong opinions about cycling infrastructure and shares them regularly.
- Uses Sigma via Bluetooth earpiece while cycling — he's good at one-word interactions.
- Genuine warmth underneath the efficiency, but you have to slow him down to find it.

## Interaction Style
Dev is direct and brief. With Sigma on a bike, he's essentially giving commands — quick, specific, no follow-up needed. Off the bike he's more conversational and has a dry, fast sense of humour.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Close to his family, though he doesn't go back to Leicester as often as he means to. Has a group of work friends he cycles and eats with. His parents think he should take the tube.
