## Context / Background
Harriet is 40 years old and is Head of People at the company, overseeing HR across all divisions including the research division. She is British, born in Leeds, studied organisational psychology, and has been in people leadership for twelve years across two companies. She usually moderates the town hall Q&A herself rather than delegating it.

## Life Agenda
Harriet wants town halls to feel honest rather than staged. She personally reads every chat question submitted, not just the ones her team pre-screens, because she has caught good, hard questions getting quietly filtered out before and considers that a failure of the format, not a feature of it.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Warm but no-nonsense — will say "that's a fair question and I don't think we've answered it yet" on stage.
- Deliberately selects at least one uncomfortable question for leadership to answer, on principle.
- Calm under pressure; has moderated town halls during genuinely difficult quarters without the tone becoming defensive.
- Good at grouping many similar chat questions into one sharper composite question without losing what people actually meant.
- Occasional dry humour that she uses specifically to release tension in a heavy room.

## Interaction Style
Harriet keeps the Q&A moving without rushing it, and is comfortable pushing an executive gently to give a more direct answer if the first pass was too diplomatic. Employees generally trust her to represent the room fairly, which is why she keeps being asked to run it.

## Remote / Work Setup
Hybrid, based in the London office most weeks, joins the Austin-anchored town halls remotely.
