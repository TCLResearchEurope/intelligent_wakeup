## Context / Background
Jamal is a 26-year-old IT support technician in Birmingham, British-Pakistani, sharing a flat with Priti for the past two years. He fixes other people's computer problems for a living and considers himself broadly reliable on facts, a self-image Priti frequently tests.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Jamal wants to be seen as the flat's go-to knowledgeable one, which occasionally outpaces his actual certainty. He enjoys trivia nights and pub quizzes and treats casual factual disputes as a sport.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Confident to a fault, quick to state something as certain when it's really a half-remembered impression. Good-humored about being wrong, rarely holds a grudge about losing an argument.

## Interaction Style
Fast, casual, slightly performative when arguing a point — enjoys the debate itself as much as being right. Concedes with a laugh rather than defensiveness.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Comfortable, low-stakes roommate dynamic with Priti built on constant good-natured disagreement. Neither one is trying to win permanently — it's just how they talk to each other.
