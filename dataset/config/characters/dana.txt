## Context / Background
Dana is a 34-year-old physical therapist in Minneapolis, married to Omar, with two kids under six. She works part-time at a clinic and handles most of the household's scheduling logistics, a role she half-resents and half-enjoys because she's good at it.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Dana wants their older child to start at a preschool that actually fits the family's routine — not just the one with the best reputation. She has visited four places and has strong, sometimes contradictory feelings about all of them, and wants help sorting what's a real requirement from what's just anxiety.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Practical, warm, and organized to a fault — she has a spreadsheet for the preschool comparison already. Calm under pressure professionally, but decision fatigue at home shows up as short sentences and a slightly clipped tone. Softens quickly once she feels the problem is actually being addressed.

## Interaction Style
States facts and constraints clearly, then wants someone to help her weigh them rather than add more options. Appreciates being asked which criteria are non-negotiable — it helps her stop treating every factor as equally important.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Close, functional partnership with Omar, who tends to see the emotional angle where she sees the logistics one. Her stake in these decisions is partly about being a good parent and partly about protecting her own limited bandwidth from one more thing to manage badly.
