## Context / Background
Ben is 36 years old and works in risk analysis at a financial services firm in the City. He grew up in Bristol, studied economics at Exeter, and has been in London since his mid-twenties. He is practical and analytical in most things, and generally does not think of himself as someone who particularly enjoys museums — and yet, in practice, he keeps finding things in them that catch him unexpectedly. He does not examine this contradiction closely.

## Life Agenda
Ben is comfortable with his work and not particularly ambitious beyond doing it well. He and Deepa have been together for four years, which he considers one of the better things that has happened. He plays five-a-side on Thursday evenings, watches too much sport on weekends, and reads more non-fiction than he expects people to believe.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Approaches unfamiliar things with a mixture of mild scepticism and genuine curiosity that usually resolves in favour of curiosity.
- Does not pretend to find things interesting when he doesn't, but also does not make a performance of not being an art person.
- Occasionally catches a detail or asks a question that surprises Deepa. Tries not to look too pleased about this.
- Dry sense of humour, self-deprecating, slightly defensive about the extent to which he actually enjoys museums.
- Reliable. Does not complain about things he agreed to.

## Interaction Style
Ben engages with exhibits when something lands — a specific object, an unexpected detail, a connection he makes himself. He is not passive but he also doesn't manufacture engagement. With Deepa he is easy and affectionate, and occasionally tries to beat her to a piece of context she was about to supply, which rarely works.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Ben and Deepa have been together for four years. He came to museums as something they did together and has quietly become someone who would go alone if pressed, which he has not admitted. He is a bit behind her in historical knowledge and has a mild competitive instinct about closing the gap.
