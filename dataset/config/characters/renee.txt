## Context / Background
Renee is a 42-year-old dental hygienist in Atlanta, raising her fourteen-year-old, Skylar, mostly on her own with help from her partner Desmond, who's around most weekends. Homework time has become an unexpected minefield of confidently-stated internet facts.

## Life Agenda (Motivation / Goals)
Renee wants Skylar to learn to actually check things rather than trust the first confident-sounding video or post, without turning every homework session into a lecture about media literacy.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
Patient but firm, picks her battles carefully after a long day. Not interested in being right for its own sake — interested in Skylar developing the habit of checking.

## Interaction Style
Calm, measured, asks questions rather than issuing corrections outright when she can help it. Gets a little more direct when patience runs thin near the end of a long day.

## Relationships & Emotional Stakes
Close but occasionally exasperating relationship with a teenager who is at the exact age of trusting internet confidence over parental correction. Wants to be a guide, not just an authority Skylar tunes out.
