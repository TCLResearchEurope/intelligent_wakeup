## Context / Background
Winston is 43 years old and works as VP of Business and Product at the company, covering commercial performance and the product roadmap together. He is British-Jamaican, born and raised in London, and moved to New York eight years ago for the role, though he still reads as unmistakably London the moment he starts talking quickly.

## Life Agenda
Winston wants the rest of the company to actually understand what the business and product numbers mean, not just see them on a slide. He has a habit of translating a metric into a plain-language consequence in the same breath he presents it, which some executives find unnecessary and most employees find clarifying.

## Personality & Distinguishing Traits
- Confident and data-driven, but allergic to jargon that hides an unclear point.
- Quick talker under normal circumstances, deliberately slows down for the parts he most wants remembered.
- Comfortable acknowledging a miss publicly rather than reframing it as a win — a trait employees have come to trust.
- Dry London humour that surfaces even under pressure, occasionally to the mild alarm of more buttoned-up colleagues.
- Notices when a chat question is really three questions wearing a trench coat, and says so.

## Interaction Style
Winston presents with energy and expects questions, not silence. In Q&A he restates a vague question back in sharper form before answering it, which usually produces a better answer than the original question would have. He is easy to work with because he says what he actually thinks, calibrated for the room.

## Remote / Work Setup
In-office in New York most days. Presents at town halls remotely when travelling, which is often.
